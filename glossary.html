📚 Islamic Glossary

وَعَلَّمَ آدَمَ الْأَسْمَاءَ كُلَّهَا

"And He taught Adam the names — all of them." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:31)

Adhan أَذَان
The Islamic call to prayer, recited by the mu'adhdhin before each of the five obligatory prayers.
Adhkar أَذْكَار
Remembrances of Allah, recited at specific times (morning, evening, after salah). Includes tasbih, tahmid, takbir, and other du'as.
Arafah عَرَفَة
A plain near Makkah where pilgrims stand from noon to sunset on 9th Dhul-Hijjah. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Hajj is Arafah." The most important day of Hajj.
Ayatul Kursi آيَة ٱلْكُرْسِيّ
Verse 2:255 of Surah Al-Baqarah, describing Allah's greatness. The Prophet (ﷺ) said it is the greatest verse in the Quran. Reciting it after prayers guarantees Paradise (except death).
Dua / Du'a دُعَاء
Supplication or prayer to Allah. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Dua is worship." It is the believer's most powerful tool.
Hajj حَجّ
The major pilgrimage to Makkah, obligatory once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able. Performed during 8th-13th Dhul-Hijjah. The fifth pillar of Islam.
Halq حَلْق
Shaving the head completely after completing Hajj or Umrah. Preferred for men over trimming (taqsir).
Ihram إِحْرَام
The sacred state of purity for Hajj or Umrah. Men wear two white unstitched sheets; women wear modest dress. Certain acts become prohibited (perfume, marital relations, covering head for men).
Istighfar ٱسْتِغْفَار
Seeking forgiveness from Allah. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever constantly seeks forgiveness, Allah will make a way out of every distress, relief from every anxiety, and provide from where he does not expect."
Janazah جَنَازَة
Funeral prayer (Salat al-Janazah) for the deceased. It is a collective obligation (fard kifayah) upon the Muslim community.
Kaaba ٱلْكَعْبَة
The cube-shaped building in Makkah, Masjid al-Haram. Muslims face it during prayers (qibla). It is the House of Allah, built by Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and his son Isma'il (AS).
Mahram مَحْرَم
A male relative whom a woman cannot marry (father, brother, son, uncle, nephew). A woman requires a mahram to travel for Hajj or Umrah.
Miqat مِيقَات
Designated boundary points around Makkah where pilgrims enter the state of Ihram. Includes Dhul Hulayfah, Juhfah, Qarn al-Manazil, Yalamlam, and Dhat Irq.
Mina مِنَىٰ
A tent city near Makkah where pilgrims stay during the days of Tashreeq (11th-13th Dhul-Hijjah) for stoning the Jamarat.
Muzdalifah مُزْدَلِفَة
An open area between Arafah and Mina where pilgrims spend the night of 9th-10th Dhul-Hijjah, praying Maghrib and Isha together, and collecting pebbles for stoning.
Qibla قِبْلَة
The direction Muslims face during prayer — towards the Kaaba in Makkah.
Rabbana Dua رَبَّنَا دُعَاء
Quranic supplications that begin with "Rabbana" (Our Lord). There are 40 such du'as in the Quran, taught by the prophets and believers.
Rami رَمْي
The stoning ritual in Mina, where pilgrims throw seven small pebbles at each of the three Jamarat (pillars) representing Shaytan.
Sa'i سَعْي
Walking seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah in Makkah, commemorating Hagar's search for water for her son Isma'il. Part of both Hajj and Umrah.
Sunnah سُنَّة
The teachings, actions, sayings, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). The second primary source of Islamic law after the Quran.
Talbiyah تَلبِيَة
The pilgrim's response to Allah's call: "Labbaik Allahumma labbaik..." Recited from entering Ihram until stoning (for Hajj) or until Tawaf (for Umrah).
Taqsir تَقْصِير
Trimming the hair (for men) or cutting a fingertip's length (for women) after completing Hajj or Umrah. For men, shaving (halq) is preferred.
Tasbih تَسْبِيح
The act of glorifying Allah saying "SubhanAllah" (Glory be to Allah). Often done in sets of 33 or 100 times using prayer beads or fingers.
Tawaf طَوَاف
Circumambulation (walking in circles) around the Kaaba seven times. Performed in Hajj (Tawaf al-Ifadah, Tawaf al-Wada), Umrah, and as a separate act of worship.
Umrah عُمْرَة
The minor pilgrimage to Makkah, consisting of Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, and Halq/Taqsir. Can be performed any time of year. Highly recommended but not obligatory.
Wuquf وُقُوف
"Standing" — the act of standing in prayer and supplication at Arafah on 9th Dhul-Hijjah. The most important ritual of Hajj.
Zamzam زَمْزَم
The blessed well in Makkah, miraculously sprang for Hagar and Isma'il. Its water is blessed and healing.
