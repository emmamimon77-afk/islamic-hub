🕌 The Complete Umrah Guide

"And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah."

— Surah Al-Baqarah 2:196

Umrah is the "minor pilgrimage" — a beautiful act of worship that can be performed at any time of the year. While not obligatory like Hajj, it carries immense reward and spiritual purification. This guide will walk you through everything from preparation to completion, based on authentic sources including Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah and Sahih Hadith.

📋 Prerequisites for Umrah

While Umrah is not obligatory, it is a sacred act of worship. These conditions must be met for your Umrah to be valid and accepted.

💰 Financial Prerequisite

  • Debt-free (for obligatory Umrah): If you are performing an obligatory Umrah (vow/nadhr), all major debts must be cleared.
  • Sufficient funds for self: Transportation, accommodation, food, medical emergencies, and return ticket home.
  • Sufficient funds for dependents left at home: Your family (spouse, children, elderly parents under your care) must have enough money for living expenses while you are away.
  • Halal (lawful) earnings: The money used for Umrah must be from halal sources. Haram income invalidates the pilgrimage.
  • No intention of begging: A pilgrim cannot beg during Umrah. If funds run out, Umrah becomes questionable.

🤝 Social Prerequisite (Rights of Others)

  • Mahram for women: A woman must travel with her husband or a male relative she cannot marry (father, brother, son, uncle).
  • No oppression (dhulm): If you have oppressed anyone — taken their money, hurt their reputation, physically harmed them — Umrah is not accepted until you return their rights or seek forgiveness.
  • Seeking forgiveness: Before departure, sincerely apologize to anyone you have wronged.
  • Good character: No arguing, insulting, raising voice, pushing, or showing arrogance during Umrah.
  • No showing off (riya'): Performing Umrah for photos, status, or social media invalidates sincerity.

🕋 Spiritual Prerequisite (Rights of Allah)

  • Sincere intention (ikhlas): The heart must be free from seeking praise, wealth, or status. Only Allah's pleasure matters.
  • Knowledge before action: Learning the rituals (Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Halq/Taqsir) is essential before going.
  • Repentance (tawbah): Sincere turning away from all sins — major and minor.
  • Returning stolen property: If you have taken anything that does not belong to you, you must return it or be forgiven by the owner.
  • No arrogance: Competing in wealth (better hotels, clothes) or status during Umrah contradicts its spirit of humility.

📚 Sources: Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah (The Rites of Hajj and 'Umrah Booklet), Fiqh us Sunnah (Sayyid Sabiq), and rulings from Shaykh Ibn Uthaymin.

⚠️ Important Note: Umrah performed with haram wealth, while oppressing others, or without sincerity may be physically completed but spiritually rejected. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Allah is Good and accepts only that which is good."

🕋 Umrah vs Hajj: Key Differences

Umrah (Minor Pilgrimage)
  • ✅ Can be done any time of year
  • ✅ Shorter rituals (hours, not days)
  • ✅ Only 4 rituals: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Halq/Taqsir
  • ❌ Not obligatory (but highly recommended)
Hajj (Major Pilgrimage)
  • ✅ Specific dates (8th-13th Dhul-Hijjah)
  • ✅ Longer rituals (5-6 days)
  • ✅ 10+ rituals including Arafah, Muzdalifah, Mina
  • ✅ Obligatory once in a lifetime if able

📌 Quick Facts About Umrah

🕋 Ruling: Highly recommended (Sunnah Mu'akkadah) — not obligatory
📅 Time: Any time of year (except during Hajj days)
⏱️ Duration: 2-4 hours (depending on crowds)
🕊️ Reward: Expiation of sins between Umrah and the next
👤 Mahram: Women require a mahram (husband or male relative)
📍 Location: Makkah — Masjid al-Haram
📜 Hadith on Umrah: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Umrah is an expiation for the sins committed between it and the next Umrah."
— (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)
