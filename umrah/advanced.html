🕌 Umrah: Advanced Fiqh & Spiritual Realities

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:196)
وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلَّهِ
"And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah."

⚖️ 1. Fiqh Differences on Umrah (Madhhab Comparisons)

Is Umrah Obligatory?

  • Hanafi: Sunnah (highly recommended, not obligatory)
  • Maliki: Sunnah (recommended)
  • Shafi'i: Fard al-kifayah (sufficient if some perform it once in a lifetime)
  • Hanbali: Fard 'ayn (obligatory once in a lifetime like Hajj)

Does Umrah Expire?

  • All schools agree: You can perform Umrah multiple times. Each Umrah is independent and expiates sins between them.
📜 حَدِيث / HADITH
ٱلْعُمْرَةُ إِلَى ٱلْعُمْرَةِ كَفَّارَةٌ لِمَا بَيْنَهُمَا
Al-'umratu ila al-'umrati kaffaratun lima baynahuma.
"Umrah to the next Umrah is an expiation for the sins committed between them." — (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

💎 2. The Spiritual Depths of Umrah (Tazkiyah)

▪️ Ihram = Death to Worldly Desires

The white sheets remind the pilgrim of death. Leaving behind adornments, perfume, and stitched clothing symbolizes detachment from dunya (world).

▪️ Tawaf = The Heart's Circumambulation

Just as the body circles the Kaaba, the heart must circle only Allah — free from love of fame, wealth, status, and false gods.

▪️ Sa'i = Trusting Allah After Action

Hagar ran desperately, then stopped and trusted Allah. The lesson: Take action, then rely on Allah completely (Tawakkul).

▪️ Halq = Humility and Equality

Shaving the head removes distinctions of wealth, race, and status. All pilgrims become equal before Allah.

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-HASHR (59:19)
وَلَا تَكُونُوا كَالَّذِينَ نَسُوا اللَّهَ فَأَنْسَاهُمْ أَنْفُسَهُمْ ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْفَاسِقُونَ
"And do not be like those who forgot Allah, so He made them forget themselves. Those are the defiantly disobedient."

👩 3. Women-Specific Rulings for Umrah

Mahram Requirement:

  • A woman must travel with her husband or a male relative she cannot marry (father, brother, son, uncle, nephew).
  • The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "A woman should not travel except with a mahram." (Bukhari)

Ihram Clothing for Women:

  • Any modest clothing that covers the body (except face and hands)
  • No niqab (face veil) or gloves — face and hands remain uncovered in Ihram
  • Can use an umbrella or visor to shield face from sun

Menstruation (Hayd) During Umrah:

  • If a woman menstruates before entering Ihram: She must wait until clean to enter Ihram.
  • If menstruation begins after Ihram: She cannot perform Tawaf until clean. She can perform Sa'i and other rituals.
  • She remains in Ihram until she performs Tawaf after becoming clean.
📜 حَدِيث / HADITH
عَنْ عَائِشَةَ رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهَا قَالَتْ: خَرَجْنَا مَعَ ٱلنَّبِيِّ صَلَّى ٱللَّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لَا نُرَى إِلَّا ٱلْحَجَّ، فَلَمَّا كُنَّا بِسَرِفَ حِضْتُ، فَدَخَلَ عَلَيَّ ٱلنَّبِيُّ صَلَّى ٱللَّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ وَأَنَا أَبْكِي، قَالَ: «مَا لَكِ؟ نُفِسْتِ؟» قُلْتُ: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: «هَذَا شَيْءٌ كَتَبَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ عَلَىٰ بَنَاتِ آدَمَ، فَٱفْعَلِي مَا يَفْعَلُ ٱلْحَاجُّ غَيْرَ أَنْ لَا تَطُوفِي بِٱلْبَيْتِ حَتَّىٰ تَطْهُرِي»
'An 'A'ishah radiya Allahu 'anha qalat: Kharajna ma'an-nabiyyi sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam la nara illal hajj. Falamma kunna bi-Sarif hi'dtu, fadakhala 'alayyan-nabiyyu sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam wa ana abki, qala: "Ma laki? Nufisti?" Qultu: Na'am. Qala: "Hadha shay'un katabahullahu 'ala banati Adama, faf'ali ma yaf'alul-hajju ghayra an la tatawfi bil-bayti hatta tatahhari."
Aisha (RA) said: "We set out with the Prophet (ﷺ) intending only Hajj. When we reached Sarif, I menstruated. The Prophet (ﷺ) entered upon me while I was crying. He said: 'What is wrong? Have you menstruated?' I said: 'Yes.' He said: 'This is something Allah has decreed for the daughters of Adam. Do what the pilgrim does, except do not perform Tawaf around the House until you become clean.'" — (Bukhari, Muslim)

🕋 4. Performing Umrah on Behalf of Others

Can you perform Umrah for someone else?

  • Deceased: Permitted. You may perform Umrah and gift the reward to a deceased relative or friend.
  • Living but permanently unable (elderly, sick): Permitted. You may perform Umrah on their behalf with their consent and expenses.
  • Living and able: Not permitted. Each person must perform their own Umrah.
📜 حَدِيث / HADITH
أَنَّ ٱمْرَأَةً قَالَتْ: يَا رَسُولَ ٱللَّهِ، إِنَّ أُمِّي نَذَرَتْ أَنْ تَحُجَّ فَلَمْ تَحُجَّ حَتَّىٰ مَاتَتْ، أَفَأَحُجُّ عَنْهَا؟ قَالَ: «نَعَمْ، حُجِّي عَنْهَا، أَرَأَيْتِ لَوْ كَانَ عَلَىٰ أُمِّكِ دَيْنٌ أَكُنْتِ قَاضِيَةً؟ ٱقْضُوا ٱللَّٰهَ فَٱللَّٰهُ أَحَقُّ بِٱلْوَفَاءِ»
Anna imra'atan qalat: Ya Rasulallah, inna ummi nadharat an tahujja falam tahujja hatta matat, afa'hujju 'anha? Qala: "Na'am, hujji 'anha. Ara'ayti law kana 'ala ummiki daynun akunti qadiyatan? I'du lillah fa-Allahu ahaqqu bil-wafa."
A woman asked the Prophet (ﷺ): "My mother vowed to perform Hajj but died before doing so. Can I perform Hajj on her behalf?" He said: "Yes, perform Hajj on her behalf. If your mother had a debt, would you not pay it? Pay Allah's due, for Allah is more deserving of fulfillment." — (Bukhari)

⚠️ 5. Common Advanced-Level Mistakes

  • ❌ Believing Tawaf requires specific du'as for each circuit: No — any sincere du'a is acceptable.
  • ❌ Women covering their face in Ihram: Niqab is prohibited. Use an umbrella or visor.
  • ❌ Men wearing stitched clothing under Ihram: Nothing stitched should touch the body. Sandals are allowed.
  • ❌ Thinking perfume is allowed before Ihram intentionally: You must wash off perfume before entering Ihram.
  • ❌ Touching the Black Stone during crowded times: Don't harm others. Pointing from a distance is sufficient.
  • ❌ Performing Umrah during Hajj days for a non-pilgrim: Not permitted. Must wait until Hajj ends.
  • ❌ Neglecting the spiritual meaning: Rituals without understanding become empty physical movements.

🌟 6. Virtues and Rewards of Umrah

  • Expiation of sins: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Umrah to the next Umrah is an expiation for sins committed between them."
  • Umrah in Ramadan equals Hajj: The Prophet (ﷺ) said to a woman: "When Ramadan comes, perform Umrah, for Umrah in Ramadan is equivalent to Hajj with me."
  • Removal of poverty: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Perform Hajj and Umrah consecutively, for they remove poverty and sin as the bellows remove impurities from iron." (Tirmidhi)
📜 حَدِيث / HADITH
تَابِعُوا بَيْنَ ٱلْحَجِّ وَٱلْعُمْرَةِ، فَإِنَّهُمَا يَنْفِيَانِ ٱلْفَقْرَ وَٱلذُّنُوبَ كَمَا يَنْفِي ٱلْكِيرُ خَبَثَ ٱلْحَدِيدِ
Tabi'u baynal-hajji wal-'umrati, fa-innahuma yunfiyani al-faqra wadh-dhunuba kama yunfil-kiru khabathal-hadidi.
"Perform Hajj and Umrah consecutively, for they remove poverty and sin as the bellows remove impurities from iron." — (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Sunan al-Nasa'i)

📱 7. Modern Fiqh Issues for Umrah

📸 Taking Photos/Selfies:

  • Ruling: Disliked (makruh) during rituals — distracts from sincerity and worship.
  • Prohibited: If it leads to showing off (riya') or delays rituals.

💉 Vaccinations & Medical Needs:

  • Permitted — do not break Ihram unless they contain perfume.

🚗 Using Wheelchairs & Electric Carts:

  • Permitted for those with genuine need (elderly, sick).
  • Not preferred for healthy people — walking Tawaf and Sa'i is more virtuous.

📱 Using Apps and GPS for Tawaf counting:

  • Permitted but not required — be mindful not to be distracted from worship.
