📚 Level 2 of 3 — Intermediate (Step-by-Step Rituals with Du'as)

🕌 Umrah: Complete Step-by-Step Ritual Guide

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-HAJJ (22:27)
وَأَذِّن فِي النَّاسِ بِالْحَجِّ يَأْتُوكَ رِجَالًا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٍ يَأْتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٍ
"And proclaim to the people the Hajj — they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel, from every distant pass."

🕋 Umrah consists of 4 main rituals that must be performed in order:

Ihram → Tawaf → Sa'i → Halq/Taqsir

📍 STEP 1: Entering Ihram (The Sacred State)

Before Ihram:

  • Trim nails, remove unwanted hair
  • Perform ghusl (full bath) or wudu (ablution)
  • Men: Wear two white, unstitched sheets (Izar and Rida)
  • Women: Wear modest clothing (no niqab or gloves in Ihram)
  • Apply non-scented oil (if needed)

At the Miqat (boundary point):

  • Pray 2 rak'ahs of Sunnah (optional but recommended)
  • Make intention for Umrah
📜 INTENTION FOR IHRAM
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ ٱلْعُمْرَةَ فَيَسِّرْهَا لِي وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّي
Allahumma inni uridul 'umrata fayassirha li wa taqabbalha minni.
"O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah, so make it easy for me and accept it from me."

Recite the Talbiyah frequently:

📜 TALBIYAH
لَبَّيْكَ ٱللَّٰهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ ٱلْحَمْدَ وَٱلنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَٱلْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbaik Allahumma labbaik, labbaik la sharika laka labbaik, innal hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.
"Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Verily all praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty, You have no partner."
⚠️ Prohibited in Ihram: Clipping nails, cutting hair, using perfume, marital relations, hunting, wearing stitched clothes (men), covering face (women).

📍 STEP 2: Tawaf (Circumambulation of the Kaaba)

How to Perform Tawaf:

  • Enter Masjid al-Haram with right foot, saying Bismillah
  • Stop reciting Talbiyah when you see the Kaaba
  • Start at the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad)
  • Make 7 circuits (rounds) around the Kaaba
  • Men: Perform Idtiba (right shoulder uncovered) and Raml (fast walking) for first 3 rounds
  • After completing 7 rounds, pray 2 rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim (if possible)

Du'a between the Yemeni Corner and Black Stone:

📜 DU'A DURING TAWAF
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي ٱلْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina adhaban-nar.
"Our Lord, give us in this world good, and in the Hereafter good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire." — (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)
💎 Note: There is no specific du'a required for each circuit. You may make any sincere du'a in Arabic or your own language.

📍 STEP 3: Sa'i (Walking Between Safa and Marwah)

How to Perform Sa'i:

  • Go to Safa hill (now a raised platform within Masjid al-Haram)
  • Face the Kaaba, raise hands, make du'a
  • Walk to Marwah (green lights mark the distance)
  • Men jog/run between the green lights (where Hagar ran)
  • Walk normally the rest of the way
  • This counts as 1 lap (Safa → Marwah = 1 lap)
  • Return from Marwah to Safa = 2nd lap
  • Continue until you complete 7 laps (ending at Marwah)

Du'a upon reaching Safa:

📜 DU'A AT SAFA & MARWAH
إِنَّ ٱلصَّفَا وَٱلْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَآئِرِ ٱللَّهِ
Innas-safa wal-marwata min sha'a'irillah.
"Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah." — (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:158)
📜 Historical Significance: Sa'i commemorates Hagar (Hajar), the wife of Prophet Ibrahim, running seven times between Safa and Marwah searching for water for her son Isma'il. Allah then caused the well of Zamzam to spring forth.

📍 STEP 4: Halq or Taqsir (Shaving or Trimming Hair)

For Men:

  • Halq (shaving head completely): Recommended and more virtuous
  • Taqsir (trimming hair): Permitted, but shaving is better
  • Shave or trim the entire head

For Women:

  • Taqsir only: Cut a fingertip's length (about 1-2 cm) from the end of their hair
  • Not required to shave or cut all hair
📜 سورة الفتح / SURAH AL-FATH (48:27)
لَّقَدْ صَدَقَ ٱللَّهُ رَسُولَهُ ٱلرُّؤْيَا بِٱلْحَقِّ ۖ لَتَدْخُلُنَّ ٱلْمَسْجِدَ ٱلْحَرَامَ إِن شَآءَ ٱللَّهُ آمِنِينَ مُحَلِّقِينَ رُءُوسَكُمْ وَمُقَصِّرِينَ لَا تَخَافُونَ ۚ فَعَلِمَ مَا لَمْ تَعْلَمُوا۟ فَجَعَلَ مِن دُونِ ذَٰلِكَ فَتْحًا قَرِيبًا
Laqad sadaqallahu rasulahu ar-ru'ya bil-haqq. Latadkhulunnal masjidal harama in sha'allahu amineena muhalliqeeena ru'oosakum wa muqassireena la takhafoon. Fa'alima ma lam ta'lamoo fa ja'ala min dooni dhalika fathan qareeba.
"Indeed, Allah will certainly fulfill His Messenger's vision in truth. You will surely enter the Sacred Mosque, if Allah wills, in safety, with your heads shaved or hair shortened, without fear. But He knew what you did not know, so He arranged before that a near victory." — Surah Al-Fath (48:27)
✅ After Halq/Taqsir, your Umrah is complete. All prohibitions of Ihram are lifted. You may now wear normal clothes, use perfume, etc.

✅ Umrah Quick Checklist

☐ 1. Enter Ihram at Miqat
☐ 2. Recite Talbiyah frequently
☐ 3. Enter Masjid al-Haram
☐ 4. Perform Tawaf (7 circuits)
☐ 5. Pray 2 rak'ahs at Maqam Ibrahim
☐ 6. Drink Zamzam water
☐ 7. Perform Sa'i (7 laps)
☐ 8. Shave or trim hair (Halq/Taqsir)

⚠️ Common Mistakes in Umrah

  • ❌ Not performing Tawaf correctly: Must be 7 complete circuits, starting and ending at the Black Stone.
  • ❌ Touching the Kaaba unnecessarily: Don't push or harm others trying to touch it.
  • ❌ Wearing perfume in Ihram: Any scented products are prohibited.
  • ❌ Women covering face in Ihram: Niqab is not allowed; use a visor or umbrella.
  • ❌ Thinking Sa'i is from Marwah to Safa: No — it starts at Safa and ends at Marwah.
  • ❌ Reciting specific du'as for each Tawaf circuit: No authentic du'as are prescribed — make your own sincere supplications.
