🕊️ Supplications for the Dying, the Deceased, and the Bereaved

Funeral Du'as

كُلُّ نَفْسٍ ذَائِقَةُ الْمَوْتِ ۗ وَإِنَّمَا تُوَفَّوْنَ أُجُورَكُمْ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ

"Every soul will taste death. And you will only be given your full reward on the Day of Resurrection."

— Surah Al-Imran (3:185)

📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (916)
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ
"There is no god worthy of worship except Allah."
💡 Practice: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Prompt your dying ones to say 'La ilaha illallah'." (Sahih Muslim) — Do so gently, without forcing.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (6510)
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ، وَاعْفُ عَنْهُ وَأَكْرِمْ نُزُلَهُ، وَوَسِّعْ مَدْخَلَهُ، وَاغْسِلْهُ بِالْمَاءِ وَالثَّلْجِ وَالْبَرَدِ، وَنَقِّهِ مِنَ الْخَطَايَا كَمَا نَقَّيْتَ الثَّوْبَ الْأَبْيَضَ مِنَ الدَّنَسِ، وَأَبْدِلْهُ دَارًا خَيْرًا مِنْ دَارِهِ، وَأَهْلًا خَيْرًا مِنْ أَهْلِهِ، وَزَوْجًا خَيْرًا مِنْ زَوْجِهِ، وَأَدْخِلْهُ الْجَنَّةَ، وَأَعِذْهُ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ وَمِنْ عَذَابِ النَّارِ
"O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, pardon him, honor his resting place, make his entrance wide, wash him with water, snow, and hail, cleanse him of sins as a white garment is cleansed of dirt, exchange his home for a better home, his family for a better family, his spouse for a better spouse, admit him to Paradise, and protect him from the punishment of the grave and the Fire."
💡 For the deceased: This was the du'a the Prophet (ﷺ) recited for a companion who died. (Sahih Muslim)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (918)
إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ، اللَّهُمَّ أْجُرْنِي فِي مُصِيبَتِي وَأَخْلِفْ لِي خَيْرًا مِنْهَا
"To Allah we belong and to Him we return. O Allah, reward me for my affliction and give me something better than it in exchange."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) taught this du'a for any calamity, including death. Allah will replace the loss with something better.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (963)
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا. اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ أَحْيَيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَأَحْيِهِ عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، وَمَنْ تَوَفَّيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَتَوَفَّهُ عَلَى الْإِيمَانِ
"O Allah, forgive our living and our dead, those present and those absent, our young and our old, our males and our females. O Allah, whomever You keep alive from among us, keep him alive upon Islam, and whomever You cause to die from among us, cause him to die upon faith."
💡 Janazah Prayer (Salat al-Janazah): This comprehensive du'a is recited for all believers. The Prophet (ﷺ) taught it.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (3201) — SAHIH
اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ فُلَانَ بْنَ فُلَانٍ فِي ذِمَّتِكَ، وَحَبْلِ جِوَارِكَ، فَقِهِ فِتْنَةَ الْقَبْرِ وَعَذَابَ النَّارِ، وَأَنْتَ أَهْلُ الْوَفَاءِ وَالْحَقِّ، فَاغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ
"O Allah, so-and-so (name the deceased) is under Your protection and in Your care. Protect him from the trial of the grave and the punishment of the Fire. You are worthy of fidelity and truth. Forgive him and have mercy on him, for You are the Oft-Forgiving, the Most Merciful."
💡 Recite this du'a in the Janazah prayer, mentioning the deceased's name. The Prophet (ﷺ) would recite it for those who died.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (975)
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الدِّيَارِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ لَاحِقُونَ، نَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ لَنَا وَلَكُمُ الْعَافِيَةَ
"Peace be upon you, O believing and Muslim dwellers of these graves. Indeed, we will — if Allah wills — join you. We ask Allah for well-being for us and for you."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) taught this du'a when visiting graves. It acknowledges the reality of death and prays for both the living and the deceased.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (974)
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ دَارَ قَوْمٍ مُؤْمِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ لَاحِقُونَ
"Peace be upon you, abode of a believing people. We will — if Allah wills — join you."
💡 A shorter version of the grave-visiting du'a. The Prophet (ﷺ) would say this when passing by graves.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:155-157)
الَّذِينَ إِذَا أَصَابَتْهُمْ مُصِيبَةٌ قَالُوا إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ
"Who, when disaster strikes them, say, 'Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return.'"
💡 Allah promises that those who say this will receive blessings, mercy, and guidance. Recite it for patience in bereavement.
📖 QURAN · SURAH IBRAHIM (14:41)
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
"Our Lord, forgive me, my parents, and the believers on the Day the account is established."
💡 Prophet Ibrahim (AS) prayed this for himself, his parents, and all believers. It is especially beneficial for deceased parents.
📖 DIVINE COMFORT FOR THE BEREAVED
وَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّ الَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَمْوَاتًا ۚ بَلْ أَحْيَاءٌ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ يُرْزَقُونَ
"And do not think of those who have been killed in the cause of Allah as dead. Rather, they are alive with their Lord, receiving provision." — Surah Al-Imran (3:169)
💖 To the one who has lost someone: Death is not the end. The soul returns to its Creator. The believer's soul is in Paradise, enjoying blessings. Grieve, but do not despair. Make du'a for them, give charity on their behalf, and trust that you will meet again, by Allah's mercy.

📋 Funeral Du'as Summary

🕊️ At the moment of death: Prompt "La ilaha illallah" gently.

🕊️ Upon hearing news of death: "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un" + du'a for reward.

🕊️ Janazah Prayer: Du'a for all believers (living, dead, present, absent, young, old, male, female).

🕊️ Visiting graves: "Assalamu alaykum ahl ad-diyar..." — pray for the deceased.

🕊️ For patience: Recite Quran 2:155-157 and trust Allah's promise.

🕊️ Ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah): Give charity, pray for them, and recite Quran — the reward reaches the deceased.

📚 All narrations verified via Sunnah.com and the Holy Quran.

🤲 And Allah knows best. May Allah forgive our deceased, have mercy on them, and reunite us with them in Paradise. Ameen.
