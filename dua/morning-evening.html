🌅 Morning & Evening Adhkar

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-AHZAB (33:41-42)
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ ذِكْرًا كَثِيرًا وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا
"O you who have believed, remember Allah with much remembrance, and exalt Him morning and afternoon."
📚 This verse commands all believers to engage in regular dhikr (remembrance of Allah) throughout the day and night.
🌅 MORNING ADHKAR (After Fajr until Sunrise)
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:255) — AYATUL KURSI
اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ
"Allah — there is no deity worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what will be after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great."
📚 Recite once after Fajr and once after Maghrib. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory prayer, nothing prevents them from entering Paradise except death." (Sunan al-Nasa'i, Sahih)
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-IKHLAS (112)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ
لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
"Say, 'He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born. Nor is there to Him any equivalent.'"
📚 Recite 3 times morning and evening. Equivalent to one-third of the Quran. (Sahih al-Bukhari)
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FALAQ (113)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ
مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
وَمِنْ شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ
وَمِنْ شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ
وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ
"Say, 'I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak, from the evil of that which He created, and from the evil of darkness when it settles, and from the evil of the blowers in knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies.'"
📚 Recite 3 times morning and evening for protection from magic, evil eye, and all harmful creation.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AN-NAS (114)
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ
مَلِكِ النَّاسِ
إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ
مِنْ شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ
الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ
مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ
"Say, 'I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of the retreating whisperer, who whispers in the hearts of mankind — from among jinn and mankind.'"
📚 Recite 3 times morning and evening for protection from whispers of Shaytan and evil thoughts.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5088) — SAHIH
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
"In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
📚 Recite 3 times morning and evening. No calamity will befall you. (Authenticated by Albani)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (2708)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
"I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created."
📚 Recite 3 times morning and evening for protection from harm, evil eye, and venomous creatures.
🌙 EVENING ADHKAR (After Asr or Maghrib until Midnight)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (6316)
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْتُ وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْتُ، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ
"O Allah, by You I have entered the evening, and by You I have entered the morning, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the Resurrection."
📚 Recite in the evening. For morning, change "أَمْسَيْتُ" to "أَصْبَحْتُ".
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5069) — SAHIH
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا
"O Allah, I ask You for the goodness of this night and the goodness of what is in it, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of what is in it."
📚 A comprehensive du'a for protection and goodness throughout the night.

⏰ Daily Adhkar Summary

🌅 Morning (After Fajr): Ayatul Kursi (1×) + Surahs Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas (3× each) + Protection du'as (3× each)

🌙 Evening (After Asr/Maghrib): Same as morning, with evening version

💤 Before Sleep: Recite Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas (3× each), blow into palms, wipe over body. Recite Ayatul Kursi.

🤲 And Allah knows best. May Allah accept our remembrance.
