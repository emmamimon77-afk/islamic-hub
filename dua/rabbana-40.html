🤲 40 Quranic Supplications Beginning with "Our Lord" (رَبَّنَا)

📖 40 Rabbana Duas

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ

"And your Lord says, 'Call upon Me; I will respond to you.'"

— Surah Ghafir (40:60)

📖 SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:127)
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
"Our Lord, accept [this] from us. Indeed You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and Isma'il (AS) recited this after raising the foundations of the Kaaba.
📖 SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:128)
رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُسْلِمَةً لَكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبْ عَلَيْنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ
"Our Lord, make us Muslims [in submission] to You, and from our descendants a Muslim nation. And show us our rites and accept our repentance. Indeed, You are the Accepting of Repentance, the Merciful."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim's prayer for himself, his descendants, and the Ummah of Muhammad (ﷺ).
📖 SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:201) — MOST COMPREHENSIVE
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
"Our Lord, give us in this world good, and in the Hereafter good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."
💡 Virtue: This is one of the most comprehensive du'as. The Prophet (ﷺ) recited it frequently. (Sahih al-Bukhari)
📖 SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:250)
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا وَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
"Our Lord, pour upon us patience and plant firmly our feet and give us victory over the disbelieving people."
💡 Context: The believers with Talut (Saul) recited this before facing Goliath (Jalut) and his army.
📖 SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:286) — THREE DU'AS
رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِنْ نَسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ أَنْتَ مَوْلَانَا فَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
"Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget or err. Our Lord, lay not upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us. Our Lord, burden us not with that which we have no ability to bear. Pardon us, forgive us, have mercy upon us. You are our protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people."
💡 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah are sufficient for the night. (Sahih al-Bukhari)
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:8)
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ
"Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower."
💡 Context: This du'a asks for steadfastness on the straight path until death.
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:9)
رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ جَامِعُ النَّاسِ لِيَوْمٍ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ
"Our Lord, surely You will gather the people for a Day about which there is no doubt. Indeed, Allah does not fail in His promise."
💡 Context: A declaration of faith in the Day of Judgment and Allah's truthfulness.
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:16)
رَبَّنَا إِنَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
"Our Lord, indeed we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."
💡 A beautiful du'a for forgiveness and protection from Hellfire.
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:53)
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا بِمَا أَنْزَلْتَ وَاتَّبَعْنَا الرَّسُولَ فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ
"Our Lord, we have believed in what You revealed and have followed the messenger, so register us among the witnesses."
💡 Context: The disciples of Prophet Isa (Jesus) recited this du'a. (AS)
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:147)
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَإِسْرَافَنَا فِي أَمْرِنَا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا وَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
"Our Lord, forgive us our sins and the excess in our affairs, plant firmly our feet, and give us victory over the disbelieving people."
💡 The believers who remained steadfast after the Battle of Uhud recited this.
📖 SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:191-194)
رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَٰذَا بَاطِلًا سُبْحَانَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ · رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ مَنْ تُدْخِلِ النَّارَ فَقَدْ أَخْزَيْتَهُ ۖ وَمَا لِلظَّالِمِينَ مِنْ أَنْصَارٍ · رَبَّنَا إِنَّنَا سَمِعْنَا مُنَادِيًا يُنَادِي لِلْإِيمَانِ أَنْ آمِنُوا بِرَبِّكُمْ فَآمَنَّا ۚ رَبَّنَا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَكَفِّرْ عَنَّا سَيِّئَاتِنَا وَتَوَفَّنَا مَعَ الْأَبْرَارِ · رَبَّنَا وَآتِنَا مَا وَعَدْتَنَا عَلَىٰ رُسُلِكَ وَلَا تُخْزِنَا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ ۗ إِنَّكَ لَا تُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ
"Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly. Protect us from the Fire. · Our Lord, whoever You admit to the Fire, You have disgraced him. · Our Lord, we have heard a caller to faith, so we have believed. Forgive us our sins and cause us to die among the righteous. · Our Lord, grant us what You promised through Your messengers and do not disgrace us on the Day of Resurrection."
💡 These powerful verses are recited by those who reflect on the creation of the heavens and earth.
📖 SURAH AL-MA'IDAH (5:83)
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ
"Our Lord, we have believed, so register us among the witnesses."
💡 Context: The Christians who recognized the truth of Islam recited this.
📖 SURAH AL-A'RAF (7:23)
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا وَإِنْ لَمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."
💡 Context: Prophet Adam (AS) and Hawa (Eve) recited this after their mistake in Paradise.
📖 SURAH AL-A'RAF (7:47)
رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا مَعَ الْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ
"Our Lord, do not place us with the wrongdoing people."
💡 A du'a to avoid being associated with oppressors and wrongdoers.
📖 SURAH AL-A'RAF (7:89)
رَبَّنَا افْتَحْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَ قَوْمِنَا بِالْحَقِّ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الْفَاتِحِينَ
"Our Lord, decide between us and our people in truth, and You are the best of those who give decision."
💡 Context: Prophet Shu'ayb (AS) recited this against his disbelieving people.
📖 SURAH AL-A'RAF (7:126)
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
"Our Lord, pour upon us patience and let us die as Muslims."
💡 Context: The magicians of Pharaoh recited this after believing in Prophet Musa (Moses).
📖 SURAH YUNUS (10:85-86)
رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِلْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ · وَنَجِّنَا بِرَحْمَتِكَ مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
"Our Lord, make us not a trial for the wrongdoing people. And save us by Your mercy from the disbelieving people."
💡 A du'a for protection from persecution and being misled by others' suffering.
📖 SURAH IBRAHIM (14:38)
رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ مَا نُخْفِي وَمَا نُعْلِنُ ۗ وَمَا يَخْفَىٰ عَلَى اللَّهِ مِنْ شَيْءٍ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ
"Our Lord, indeed You know what we conceal and what we declare, and nothing is hidden from Allah on earth or in heaven."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim's (AS) prayer acknowledging Allah's complete knowledge.
📖 SURAH IBRAHIM (14:40-41)
رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِي ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ · رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
"My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and from my descendants. Our Lord, accept my supplication. · Our Lord, forgive me, my parents, and the believers on the Day the account is established."
💡 Prophet Ibrahim (AS) prayed for himself, his children, and all believers.
📖 SURAH AL-KAHF (18:10)
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
"Our Lord, grant us from Yourself mercy and prepare for us from our affair right guidance."
💡 Context: The young men of the cave (Ashab al-Kahf) recited this before fleeing persecution.
📖 SURAH TAHA (20:45)
رَبَّنَا إِنَّنَا نَخَافُ أَنْ يَفْرُطَ عَلَيْنَا أَوْ أَنْ يَطْغَىٰ
"Our Lord, indeed we fear that he will hasten against us or that he will transgress."
💡 Context: Prophet Musa (Moses) and Harun (Aaron) recited this before confronting Pharaoh.
📖 SURAH AL-MU'MINUN (23:109)
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الرَّاحِمِينَ
"Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us and have mercy upon us, and You are the best of the merciful."
💡 A humble plea for forgiveness and mercy from the Most Merciful.
📖 SURAH AL-FURQAN (25:65-66)
رَبَّنَا اصْرِفْ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَ ۖ إِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا · إِنَّهَا سَاءَتْ مُسْتَقَرًّا وَمُقَامًا
"Our Lord, avert from us the punishment of Hell. Indeed, its punishment is ever adhering. Indeed, it is evil as a settlement and residence."
💡 The servants of the Most Merciful (Ibad al-Rahman) recite this for protection from Hellfire.
📖 SURAH AL-FURQAN (25:74)
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
"Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us a leader for the righteous."
💡 One of the most beautiful du'as for righteous family and being an example to others.
📖 SURAH FATIR (35:34)
رَبَّنَا لَغَفُورٌ شَكُورٌ
"Our Lord is Forgiving and Appreciative."
💡 The people of Paradise will praise Allah with these words upon entering.
📖 SURAH GHAFIR (40:7-9)
رَبَّنَا وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ رَحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا فَاغْفِرْ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُوا وَاتَّبَعُوا سَبِيلَكَ وَقِهِمْ عَذَابَ الْجَحِيمِ · رَبَّنَا وَأَدْخِلْهُمْ جَنَّاتِ عَدْنٍ الَّتِي وَعَدْتَهُمْ وَمَنْ صَلَحَ مِنْ آبَائِهِمْ وَأَزْوَاجِهِمْ وَذُرِّيَّاتِهِمْ ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ · وَقِهِمُ السَّيِّئَاتِ ۚ وَمَنْ تَقِ السَّيِّئَاتِ يَوْمَئِذٍ فَقَدْ رَحِمْتَهُ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ هُوَ الْفَوْزُ الْعَظِيمُ
"Our Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge, so forgive those who have repented and followed Your way, and protect them from the punishment of Hellfire. Our Lord, admit them to gardens of perpetual residence which You have promised them, and whoever was righteous among their forefathers, spouses and offspring. And protect them from evil consequences. And he whom You protect from evil consequences that Day — You will have given him mercy, and that is the great attainment."
💡 The angels carry the Throne recite this du'a for the believers.
📖 SURAH AL-HASHR (59:10)
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلِإِخْوَانِنَا الَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِالْإِيمَانِ وَلَا تَجْعَلْ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلًّا لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ رَءُوفٌ رَحِيمٌ
"Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and do not place in our hearts any rancor toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind, Merciful."
💡 A du'a for unity and removing hatred from the hearts of believers.
📖 SURAH AL-MUMTAHANAH (60:4-5)
رَبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ · رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ
"Our Lord, in You we have placed trust, and to You we have turned in repentance, and to You is the final return. Our Lord, make us not a trial for those who disbelieve, and forgive us. Our Lord, indeed You are the Exalted in Might, the Wise."
💡 Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and the believers with him recited this as a declaration of disassociation from disbelief.
📖 SURAH AL-TAHRIM (66:8)
رَبَّنَا أَتْمِمْ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
"Our Lord, perfect our light for us and forgive us. Indeed, You are over all things competent."
💡 The believers will pray for their light to be completed on the Day of Judgment.

🤲 A Prayer for Acceptance

Rabbana taqabbal minna — "Our Lord, accept from us."

These 40 Quranic du'as are a gift from Allah to His servants. Memorize them, recite them, and teach them to your family.

📚 Source: Compiled from the Holy Quran, verified against Madinah Mushaf.
