🩺 Quranic Verses & Prophetic Supplications for Healing

Health & Healing Du'as

وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ مَا هُوَ شِفَاءٌ وَرَحْمَةٌ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ

"And We send down of the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers."

— Surah Al-Isra (17:82)

🤲 A SPECIAL PRAYER FOR YOUR LOVED ONES (and all the sick)
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ، اشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا
"O Allah, Lord of mankind, remove the harm. Heal, for You are the Healer. There is no healing except Your healing, a healing that leaves no illness behind."
💖 For your loved ones (and all the sick): The Prophet (ﷺ) taught this du'a for the sick, whether near or far. Recite it with full trust in Allah. May Allah grant your loved ones complete shifa' and reunite you in health and happiness. Ameen.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FATIHAH (1:1-7) — THE GREATEST HEALING
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ · الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ · الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ · مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ · إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ · اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ · صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds. The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the Straight Path — the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have earned anger, nor of those who are astray."
💡 Virtue: Surah Al-Fatihah is a cure for physical and spiritual ailments. The companions used to recite it over the sick. (Sahih al-Bukhari)
📖 QURAN · SURAH YUNUS (10:57) — HEALING FOR THE HEART
يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ قَدْ جَاءَتْكُمْ مَوْعِظَةٌ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ وَشِفَاءٌ لِمَا فِي الصُّدُورِ وَهُدًى وَرَحْمَةٌ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ
"O mankind, there has come to you instruction from your Lord and healing for what is in the hearts and guidance and mercy for the believers."
💡 The Quran heals spiritual diseases: doubt, hypocrisy, anxiety, grief, and despair.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AN-NAHL (16:69) — HONEY AS HEALING
يَخْرُجُ مِنْ بُطُونِهَا شَرَابٌ مُخْتَلِفٌ أَلْوَانُهُ فِيهِ شِفَاءٌ لِلنَّاسِ
"There emerges from their bellies a drink of varying colors, in which there is healing for people."
💡 Honey is both physical medicine (antibacterial, wound healing) and a barakah from Allah.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (5742)
أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ رَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ أَنْ يَشْفِيَكَ
"I ask Allah, the Mighty, the Lord of the Mighty Throne, to cure you."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever visits a sick person and says this seven times, Allah will cure him." (Sunan Abi Dawud, Sahih)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (2202)
أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، وَاشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا
"Remove the harm, O Lord of mankind. Cure, for You are the Healer. There is no cure except Your cure, a cure that leaves no illness behind."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) used to recite this du'a while wiping the area of pain on the sick person. (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (5675)
لَا بَأْسَ، طَهُورٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
"Do not worry. It (the sickness) will be a purification (from sins), if Allah wills."
💡 Comforting words for the sick. Sickness expiates sins and raises ranks.
📜 RUQYAH · BLOWING ON THE SICK
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ (٣ مَرَّات)
أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ وَقُدْرَتِهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا أَجِدُ وَأُحَاذِرُ (٧ مَرَّات)
"In the name of Allah (3 times). I seek refuge in Allah and His power from the evil of what I feel and fear (7 times)."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) taught this ruqyah. Recite on yourself or on the sick, blow into palms, and wipe over the body.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (2731)
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from the burden of debt and the oppression of men."
💡 A comprehensive du'a for mental and emotional health. The Prophet (ﷺ) recited this frequently.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:250) & SURAH AL-A'RAF (7:126)
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
"Our Lord, pour upon us patience and let us die as Muslims."
💡 A powerful du'a for patience (sabr) during illness and hardship, asking Allah to accept us in a state of submission.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (3107)
أَذْهَبَ اللَّهُ بَأْسَكَ، وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ، وَعَافَاكَ فِي دِينِكَ وَجِسْمِكَ
"May Allah remove your affliction, forgive your sins, and grant you well-being in your religion and body."
💡 Recite this du'a when visiting the sick. Angels say "Ameen" and the sick person's sins are forgiven.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ
"O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in this world and the Hereafter."
💡 The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "There is no du'a more comprehensive than 'Allahumma inni as'aluka al-'afwa wal-'afiyah'." (Authenticated by Albani)

🤲 A Healing Reminder

📖 Read Surah Al-Fatihah over the sick — the companions did this, and the Prophet (ﷺ) approved.

🩺 Seek medical treatment — The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Seek treatment, for Allah has sent a cure for every disease except old age and death."

💧 Honey and black seed (Kalonji) — The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "In black seed there is healing for every disease except death." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

🕋 For the distant sick: Make du'a sincerely. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "The du'a of a Muslim for his brother in his absence is readily answered." (Sahih Muslim)

📚 All narrations verified via Sunnah.com and Quran.

🤲 And Allah knows best. May Allah grant your mother shifa' kamilah (complete healing) and comfort your heart. Ameen.
