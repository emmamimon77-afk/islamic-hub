🏠 Supplications for Spouses, Parents, Children, and Righteous Offspring

Family & Children Du'as

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

"Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us a leader for the righteous."

— Surah Al-Furqan (25:74)

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FURQAN (25:74)
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
"Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us a leader for the righteous."
💡 Context: One of the most beautiful du'as for a righteous family. "Comfort to our eyes" means that seeing them brings joy and peace to the heart.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (3371)
أُعِيذُكُمَا بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ وَهَامَّةٍ وَمِنْ كُلِّ عَيْنٍ لَامَّةٍ
"I seek refuge for you both in the perfect words of Allah from every devil, every poisonous creature, and from every evil eye."
💡 Practice: The Prophet (ﷺ) used to recite this du'a for Al-Hasan and Al-Husayn and said: "Your father (Ibrahim) used to recite it for Isma'il and Ishaq." Recite this over your children daily.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FURQAN (25:74)
رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ
"My Lord, grant me from among the righteous."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim (AS) prayed this du'a for a righteous child. Allah responded by granting him Isma'il and Ishaq (AS).
📖 QURAN · SURAH IBRAHIM (14:40)
رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِي ۖ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ
"My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and from my descendants. Our Lord, accept my supplication."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim (AS) prayed for himself and his descendants to be steadfast in prayer.
📖 QURAN · SURAH IBRAHIM (14:41) & SURAH AL-ISRA (17:24)
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
"Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers the Day the account is established."
💡 Virtue: This du'a includes forgiveness for oneself, parents, and all believers. It is particularly important to pray for deceased parents.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-ISRA (17:24)
رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا
"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they raised me when I was small."
💡 Context: This is the du'a that children should make for their parents, especially after they pass away. It acknowledges the care and love parents showed.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:128)
رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُسْلِمَةً لَكَ
"Our Lord, make us Muslims [in submission] to You, and from our descendants a Muslim nation."
💡 Context: Prophet Ibrahim (AS) prayed for himself and his descendants to be submissive to Allah.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FURQAN (25:74)
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ
"Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses comfort to our eyes."
💡 A beautiful du'a for a righteous, loving spouse who brings joy and peace to the heart.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD
أُعِيذُكَ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ غَضَبِهِ وَعِقَابِهِ وَشَرِّ عِبَادِهِ وَمِنْ هَمَزَاتِ الشَّيَاطِينِ وَأَنْ يَحْضُرُونِ
"I seek refuge for you in the perfect words of Allah from His anger and punishment, from the evil of His servants, from the whispers of devils, and from their presence."
💡 Practice: Recite this du'a over your children daily for comprehensive protection.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى
"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and sufficiency."
💡 This du'a asks Allah for guidance (huda), righteousness (tuqa), protection from sin (afaf), and contentment/sufficiency (ghina) — all essential for a happy family life.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-TAHRIM (66:8)
رَبَّنَا أَتْمِمْ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا
"Our Lord, perfect our light for us and forgive us."
💡 A prayer for the light of faith to be completed in the family, and for Allah's forgiveness.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-HUJURAT (49:10)
رَبَّنَا أَلِّفْ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِنَا
"Our Lord, bring our hearts together."
💡 A beautiful du'a for unity, love, and harmony within the family. The believers are brothers, and this du'a asks Allah to strengthen that bond.

🏠 Family Du'as Summary

For Children: Recite the protection du'a of the Prophet (ﷺ) over them daily.

For Parents: Pray "Rabbirhamhuma" for them, especially after they pass away.

For Spouse: Pray for a righteous spouse who brings "coolness of the eyes."

For Righteous Offspring: Make du'a for children who establish prayer and are among the righteous.

Daily Family Du'a: "Rabbana hablana min azwajina wa dhurriyatina qurrata a'yun" (25:74)

📚 All narrations verified via Sunnah.com and Quran (Madinah Mushaf)

🤲 And Allah knows best. May Allah bless our families and make them righteous.
