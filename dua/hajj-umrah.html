🕋 Essential Supplications for the Pilgrim

Hajj & Umrah Du'as

وَأَذِّنْ فِي النَّاسِ بِالْحَجِّ يَأْتُوكَ رِجَالًا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٍ يَأْتِينَ مِنْ كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٍ

"And proclaim to the people the Hajj — they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel, from every distant pass."

— Surah Al-Hajj (22:27)

📖 INTENTION (NIYYAH) — START OF IHRAM
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ حَجًّا (لِلْعُمْرَةِ)
"Here I am, O Allah, for Hajj (or for Umrah)."
💡 At the Miqat: Enter the state of Ihram with this intention. For Hajj, say "Labbaik Allahumma Hajjan." For Umrah, say "Labbaik Allahumma Umratan."
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI & MUSLIM
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
"Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Verily all praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty. You have no partner."
💡 Practice: Recite the Talbiyah frequently from the moment you enter Ihram until you begin the stoning (for Hajj) or until you start Tawaf (for Umrah). The Prophet (ﷺ) recited it throughout his pilgrimage.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN AL-TIRMIDHI — SAHIH
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، فَحَيِّنَا رَبَّنَا بِالسَّلَامِ
"O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Greet us, our Lord, with peace."
💡 First sight: Raise your hands and make du'a. This is a moment when du'as are readily accepted. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "When you see the Kaaba, raise your hands and make du'a, for it is a time when prayers are answered."
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:201) — BEST DU'A FOR TAWAF
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
"Our Lord, give us in this world good, and in the Hereafter good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."
💡 Between the Yemeni Corner and Black Stone: The Prophet (ﷺ) would say this du'a in this specific area of Tawaf. Make your own du'as throughout the circuits.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:158) & HADITH
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ
"Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah."
💡 At Safa: Face the Kaaba, raise your hands, praise Allah, and make du'a. Then walk to Marwah. Repeat similarly at Marwah.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN AL-TIRMIDHI — THE BEST DU'A
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
"There is no god worthy of worship except Allah alone, without any partners. To Him belongs sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things competent."
💡 Day of Arafah (9th Dhul-Hijjah): The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "The best du'a is the du'a of the day of Arafah, and the best thing I and the prophets before me said is 'La ilaha illallah...'" (Tirmidhi, Sahih)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا
"O Allah, I ask You for the goodness of this night and the goodness of what is in it, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of what is in it."
💡 After Arafah, pilgrims spend the night in Muzdalifah under the open sky. Make du'a abundantly, collect pebbles, and rest before proceeding to Mina.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN IBN MAJAH
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ (عِندَ رَمْيِ كُلِّ حَصَاةٍ)
"Allah is the Greatest" (said with each pebble thrown).
💡 In Mina (10th-13th): With each pebble thrown at the Jamarat, say "Allahu Akbar." After stoning the Small and Middle Jamarat, step aside, face Qibla, and make du'a. The Great Jamarat has no du'a after it.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM
اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَجْعَلْهُ آخِرَ الْعَهْدِ مِنْ بَيْتِكَ
"O Allah, do not make this my last covenant with Your House."
💡 Before leaving Makkah, perform Farewell Tawaf. Ask Allah to grant you the ability to return again. This Tawaf is wajib (obligatory) for all pilgrims except menstruating women.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:127)
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
"Our Lord, accept [this] from us. Indeed You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
💡 After completing Hajj or Umrah, make this du'a of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and Isma'il (AS) for acceptance. Ask Allah to accept your rituals and forgive your shortcomings.

📋 Hajj & Umrah Du'as Summary

🕋 Miqat (Start): Talbiyah — "Labbaik Allahumma..."

🕋 At Kaaba: "Allahumma Antas-Salam..." — raise hands at first sight

🕋 Tawaf: "Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah..." between Yemeni Corner and Black Stone

🕋 Sa'i: Recite "Innas-Safa wal-Marwata..." at Safa and Marwah

🕋 Arafah: "La ilaha illallah wahdahu..." — the Prophet's du'a

🕋 Muzdalifah: Du'a for the night's goodness and protection

🕋 Stoning: "Allahu Akbar" with each pebble

🕋 Farewell Tawaf: "Allahumma la taj'alhu akhiral 'ahd..."

📚 All narrations verified from Quran and authentic Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Ibni Majah)

🤲 And Allah knows best. May Allah accept our pilgrimage and answer our du'as. Ameen.
