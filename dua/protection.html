🛡️ Verified Protection Du'as — From Quran & Authentic Sunnah

Protection Du'as & Ruqyah

قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
"Say, 'He is Allah, the One.'"

— Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1)

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:255) — AYATUL KURSI
ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَىُّ ٱلْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٌۭ وَلَا نَوْمٌۭ ۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِى ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ ۗ مَن ذَا ٱلَّذِى يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُۥ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِۦ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَىْءٍۢ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِۦ إِلَّا بِمَا شَآءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَـُٔودُهُۥ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْعَلِىُّ ٱلْعَظِيمُ
Meaning: "Allah — there is no deity worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence. Neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is it that can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what will be after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except for what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation tires Him not. And He is the Most High, the Most Great."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory prayer, nothing prevents them from entering Paradise except death." (Sunan al-Nasa'i, Sahih) — Recite morning and evening for comprehensive protection.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-IKHLAS (112)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌۭ
Meaning: "Say, 'He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born. Nor is there to Him any equivalent.'"
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "By the One in Whose hand is my soul, it (Al-Ikhlas) is equivalent to one-third of the Quran." (Sahih al-Bukhari) — Recite 3 times morning and evening.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-FALAQ (113)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ
مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ
وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّـٰثَـٰتِ فِى ٱلْعُقَدِ
وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ
Meaning: "Say, 'I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak, from the evil of that which He created, and from the evil of darkness when it settles, and from the evil of the blowers in knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies.'"
📚 Virtue: Protects from magic, evil eye, and all harmful creation. Recite 3 times morning and evening.
📖 QURAN · SURAH AN-NAS (114)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ
مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ
إِلَـٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ
مِن شَرِّ ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ
ٱلَّذِى يُوَسْوِسُ فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ
مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ
Meaning: "Say, 'I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of the retreating whisperer, who whispers in the hearts of mankind — from among jinn and mankind.'"
📚 Virtue: Protects from waswas (whisperings of Shaytan) and evil thoughts. Recite with Al-Falaq and Al-Ikhlas 3 times morning and evening.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5088) — SAHIH (ALBANI)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلَّذِى لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ ٱسْمِهِۦ شَىْءٌۭ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِى ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ
Meaning: "In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
📚 Virtue: Recite 3 times every morning and evening. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "No sudden calamity will befall the person who recites this three times in the morning and three times in the evening." (Verified authentic by Albani)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (3371) & SAHIH MUSLIM (2208)
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
Meaning: "I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites this three times in the evening will be protected from harm that night." (Musnad Ahmad — authenticated by Albani) — Also used for protection of children.
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (3371) — FOR HASAN & HUSAYN
أُعِيذُكُمَا بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ وَهَامَّةٍ وَمِنْ كُلِّ عَيْنٍ لَامَّةٍ
Meaning: "I seek refuge for you both in the perfect words of Allah from every devil, every poisonous/venomous creature, and from every evil eye."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) used to recite this du'a for Al-Hasan and Al-Husayn and said: "Your father (Ibrahim) used to recite it for Isma'il and Ishaq." (Sahih al-Bukhari) — Note: "Wa hammah" (وَهَامَّةٍ) refers to venomous creatures like snakes and scorpions.
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5095) & SUNAN AL-TIRMIDHI (3426)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ
Meaning: "In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except with Allah."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "It will be said to the one who says this: 'You are guided, protected, and sufficed.' And Shaytan will turn away from him." (Authenticated by Albani)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (2018) & SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5096)
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا، وَبِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا، وَعَلَىٰ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
Meaning: "In the name of Allah we enter, and in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord we rely."
📚 Virtue: Then greet your family with Salam. The Prophet (ﷺ) said that Shaytan will not enter with you. (Authenticated)
📜 HADITH · SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5080) — RECITE 7X AFTER FAJR & MAGHRIB
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ أَجِرْنِى مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ
Meaning: "O Allah, protect me from the Fire."
📚 Virtue: Recite 7 times after Fajr and Maghrib. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever asks Allah for Paradise three times, Paradise says: 'O Allah, admit him into Paradise.' Whoever seeks protection from Hellfire three times, Hellfire says: 'O Allah, protect him from me.'" (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Sahih)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (6369) & SAHIH MUSLIM (2706)
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ إِنِّى أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ ٱلْهَمِّ وَٱلْحَزَنِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ ٱلْعَجْزِ وَٱلْكَسَلِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ ٱلْجُبْنِ وَٱلْبُخْلِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ غَلَبَةِ ٱلدَّيْنِ وَقَهْرِ ٱلرِّجَالِ
Meaning: "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from the burden of debt and the oppression of men."
📚 Virtue: A comprehensive du'a for protection from psychological and financial hardships. The Prophet (ﷺ) frequently recited this. (Sahih al-Bukhari)
📜 HADITH · SAHIH MUSLIM (2202) — FOR PHYSICAL AILMENTS
أَذْهِبِ ٱلْبَأْسَ رَبَّ ٱلنَّاسِ، وَٱشْفِ أَنْتَ ٱلشَّافِى، لَا شِفَآءَ إِلَّا شِفَآؤُكَ، شِفَآءً لَّا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا
Meaning: "Remove the harm, O Lord of mankind, and cure — You are the Healer. There is no cure except Your cure, a cure that leaves no illness behind."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) recited this du'a when visiting the sick and wiping the area of pain. (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)
🌙 COMPLETE MORNING & EVENING PROTECTION
قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ (3×)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ (3×)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ (3×)
Meaning: Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas three times each, morning and evening.
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Recite 'Say: He is Allah, the One' and the two surahs of refuge (Al-Falaq and An-Nas) three times when you enter the evening and when you enter the morning — they will suffice you against everything." (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Sahih)

⏰ Daily Protection Routine (Authentic Sunnah)

🌅 Morning (After Fajr): Ayatul Kursi + Surah Al-Ikhlas (3×), Al-Falaq (3×), An-Nas (3×) + "Bismillah alladhi la yadurru..." (3×)

🌙 Evening (After Asr/Maghrib): Same as morning + "A'udhu bikalimatillah..." (3×)

🚪 Leaving Home: "Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah..."

🏠 Entering Home: "Bismillahi walajna..." + greeting family with Salam

👶 For Children: Recite the du'a of Bukhari 3371 over them daily

📚 All narrations verified via Sunnah.com | Gradings based on Shaykh Albani & major Hadith scholars
