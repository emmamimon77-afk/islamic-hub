🕌 Authentic Remembrances After Obligatory Prayers

After Salah Du'as & Adhkar

فَإِذَا قَضَيْتُمُ الصَّلَاةَ فَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ قِيَامًا وَقُعُودًا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِكُمْ

"And when you have completed the prayer, remember Allah standing, sitting, and on your sides."

— Surah An-Nisa (4:103)

📜 SUNAN ABI DAWUD — SAHIH
أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ
"I seek forgiveness from Allah."
📚 Practice: Recite 3 times after every obligatory prayer. The Prophet (ﷺ) used to seek forgiveness three times before turning around. (Sahih Muslim)
📜 SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (843) & SAHIH MUSLIM (593)
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
"O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Owner of majesty and honor."
📚 Practice: Recite immediately after finishing the prayer, before other adhkar. The Prophet (ﷺ) would turn after praying and say this. (Sahih Muslim)
📜 SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (843) & SAHIH MUSLIM (597)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ (٣٣ مرة)
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (٣٣ مرة)
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ (٣٣ مرة)
ثُمَّ يَقُولُ: لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ (مرة واحدة)
"Glory be to Allah (33 times), Praise be to Allah (33 times), Allah is the Greatest (33 times). Then say: 'There is no god worthy of worship except Allah alone, without any partners. To Him belongs sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things competent.'"
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever glorifies Allah 33 times, praises Allah 33 times, and magnifies Allah 33 times, then completes with 'La ilaha illallah...' — his sins will be forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea." (Sahih Muslim)
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:255) — AYATUL KURSI
اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ
"Allah — there is no deity worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence..."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites Ayatul Kursi after every obligatory prayer, nothing prevents them from entering Paradise except death." (Sunan al-Nasa'i, Sahih)
📜 SUNAN AL-TIRMIDHI (289) — SAHIH
قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ (١ مرة أو ٣ مرات)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ (١ مرة أو ٣ مرات)
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ (١ مرة أو ٣ مرات)
"Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas either once or three times after each prayer."
📚 Virtue: The Prophet (ﷺ) recommended reciting these three surahs after every prayer. Some scholars recommend once, others three times. (Sunan al-Tirmidhi, authenticated by Albani)
📜 SAHIH MUSLIM (2697)
اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَىٰ ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ
"O Allah, help me to remember You, to thank You, and to worship You in the best manner."
📚 The Prophet (ﷺ) taught this du'a to Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) as a comprehensive supplication.
📜 SAHIH MUSLIM (2720)
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْمَأْثَمِ وَالْمَغْرَمِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the grave, from the trial of the False Messiah (Dajjal), from the trials of life and death. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from sin and debt."
📚 The Prophet (ﷺ) frequently recited this du'a after the tashahhud before finishing prayer. It is often recited after the taslim as well.
📜 SAHIH AL-BUKHARI (6329)
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي مَا قَدَّمْتُ وَمَا أَخَّرْتُ، وَمَا أَسْرَرْتُ وَمَا أَعْلَنْتُ، وَمَا أَسْرَفْتُ، وَمَا أَنْتَ أَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنِّي، أَنْتَ الْمُقَدِّمُ وَأَنْتَ الْمُؤَخِّرُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
"O Allah, forgive me my past and future sins, my secret and open sins, my excesses, and what You know better than I. You are the One who advances and delays. There is no god worthy of worship except You."
📚 The Prophet (ﷺ) said this du'a after the tashahhud. It is a comprehensive request for forgiveness of all types of sins.
📜 SAHIH MUSLIM (597)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ (٣٣ مرة)
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (٣٣ مرة)
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ (٣٤ مرة)
"Glory be to Allah (33 times), Praise be to Allah (33 times), Allah is the Greatest (34 times)."
📚 This makes a total of 100. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever says this, their sins will be forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea." (Sahih Muslim)
📜 SUNAN ABI DAWUD (5074) — SAHIH
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي
"O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in this world and the Hereafter. O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in my religion, my worldly affairs, my family, and my wealth."
📚 A comprehensive du'a covering all aspects of life — spiritual, physical, family, and financial well-being.

⏰ After Salah Routine Summary

1. Istighfar: "Astaghfirullah" (3 times)

2. Du'a for Peace: "Allahumma Antas-Salam..." (once)

3. Tasbih: SubhanAllah (33×), Alhamdulillah (33×), Allahu Akbar (33×) + "La ilaha illallah..." (once)

4. Ayatul Kursi (once)

5. Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas (once or 3× each)

6. Additional du'as for forgiveness, protection, and well-being

📚 All narrations verified via Sunnah.com (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi)
