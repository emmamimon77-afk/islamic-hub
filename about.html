📖 About This Project

🤲 In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

This Islamic Hub was born out of a sincere desire to learn — and to share that learning with others. The journey began with a simple question:

❝ How does a believer truly perform Hajj or Umrah — not just the rituals, but the preparation of the heart, the conditions of acceptance, and the knowledge required before even setting foot on the journey? ❞

What started as a personal quest for understanding grew into a desire to compile everything in one authentic, organized, and accessible place — not for showing off, but for sincere benefit.

🕋 The Core Purpose

This hub is built on three foundational questions:

  • What must a believer learn BEFORE performing Hajj or Umrah? — The prerequisites: financial, social, and spiritual.
  • What are the conditions for acceptance? — Sincere intention, pure earnings, returning others' rights, and good character.
  • What is most important in making the decision to go? — Not wealth or status, but sincerity (ikhlas) and ability (physical and financial) without burdening oneself or others.

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Actions are judged by intentions." (Sahih al-Bukhari). This is the spirit behind every page — to remind the pilgrim that the journey begins in the heart before it begins on the road.

📚 What You Will Find Here

  • Hajj & Umrah — Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels, with quizzes to test knowledge.
  • Authentic Du'as — From the Quran and Sahih Hadith, categorized for daily life, health, grief, family, and more.
  • Protection & Adhkar — Morning, evening, and after-salah remembrances, verified from authentic sources.
  • Glossary & References — So every visitor knows where the information comes from.

⚠️ A Humble Note on Accuracy

Every Quranic verse has been verified against the Madinah Mushaf. Every Hadith has been checked against Sunnah.com and only Sahih (authentic) or Hasan (good) narrations are included. Weak or fabricated narrations have been deliberately excluded.

If any error exists — in diacritics, translation, or attribution — it is from our human limitation. Allah knows best, and to Him belongs all perfection.

🌱 A Personal Reflection from the creator of this page

"I wanted to learn everything in one place — not scattered across books and websites. As I learned, I realized that this knowledge should not remain locked within myself. It is a trust (amanah) to be shared with others who seek the same path — with sincerity, humility, and the pure intention of drawing closer to Allah."

This hub is not about perfection. It is about sincere effort (ijtihad) in the way of Allah. If it helps one person avoid a mistake in Hajj, or brings comfort through a du'a in times of distress, or inspires someone to learn — then by Allah's mercy, the effort has been worthwhile. Fatimah Khin Khin
