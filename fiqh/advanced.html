📖 Advanced Fiqh Rulings

فَإِنْ تَنَازَعْتُمْ فِي شَيْءٍ فَرُدُّوهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَالرَّسُولِ

"If you disagree over anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger." — Surah An-Nisa (4:59)

⚖️ 1. Differences Among the Four Madhhabs

Scholarly differences are a mercy. The four major schools of Islamic jurisprudence (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) are all valid paths to follow. Below are key differences in Hajj and Umrah rulings.

📜 Principle: When scholars differ, follow the opinion with strongest evidence, or follow a reliable mufti. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Differences in my Ummah are a mercy." (Al-Bayhaqi)

📱 2. Contemporary Issues (Modern Fiqh)

📸 Taking Photos/Selfies During Hajj/Umrah

  • Ruling: Disliked (makruh) during rituals — distracts from sincerity and worship.
  • Prohibited: If it leads to showing off (riya') or delays wajib acts.
📜 SCHOLARLY NOTE
The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever does an action for show, Allah will expose him." (Sahih Muslim)

💉 Vaccinations & Medical Needs

  • Injections (non-nutritional): Permitted — do not break Ihram unless they contain perfume.
  • Blood tests, IV fluids: Permitted if medically necessary.
  • Vaccinations required by Saudi law: Obligatory to comply.

🚗 Using Wheelchairs & Electric Carts

  • Permitted for those with genuine need (elderly, sick).
  • Not preferred for healthy people — walking Tawaf and Sa'i is more virtuous.

📱 Using Apps to Count Tawaf Circuits

  • Permitted but not required — be mindful not to be distracted from worship.
  • Traditional counting with fingers or tasbih beads is preferred.

🕋 3. Performing Hajj or Umrah on Behalf of Others

📖 QURAN & SUNNAH
وَأَن لَّيْسَ لِلْإِنسَانِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ
Meaning: "And that man will have nothing but what he strives for." — Surah An-Najm (53:39)

General Ruling:

  • Living and able: Not permitted — each person must perform their own Hajj.
  • Deceased: Permitted — the heir may perform Hajj on their behalf or pay someone to do so.
  • Living but permanently unable (elderly, terminally ill): Permitted — with their consent and expenses.
📜 HADITH
A woman asked the Prophet (ﷺ): "My mother vowed to perform Hajj but died before doing so. Can I perform Hajj on her behalf?" He said: "Yes, perform Hajj on her behalf. If your mother had a debt, would you not pay it? Pay Allah's due, for Allah is more deserving of fulfillment." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

👩 4. Women-Specific Rulings (Advanced)

Menstruation (Hayd) During Hajj/Umrah

يَسْأَلُونَكَ عَنِ الْمَحِيضِ ۖ قُلْ هُوَ أَذًى
Meaning: "They ask you about menstruation. Say: It is harm." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:222)
  • Before Ihram: Must wait until clean to enter Ihram.
  • After Ihram: Can perform all rituals except Tawaf.
  • If Tawaf is missed due to menses: Must wait until clean, then perform Tawaf before leaving Makkah.
  • Farewell Tawaf: Exempted for menstruating women.

Mahram Requirement

  • A woman must travel with her husband or a male relative she cannot marry (father, brother, son, uncle, nephew).
  • Some modern scholars permit travel with a trusted group of women for Umrah, but the majority require a mahram.
📜 HADITH
The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "A woman should not travel except with a mahram." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

⚠️ 5. Common Advanced-Level Mistakes

  • ❌ Thinking Tamattu' is only for certain nationalities: Tamattu' is for all pilgrims not bringing a sacrifice animal.
  • ❌ Believing stoning the pillars literally stones Shaytan: It is symbolic — a declaration of rejection of evil.
  • ❌ Neglecting the spiritual meaning: Rituals without understanding become mere physical movement.
  • ❌ Correcting others harshly: Advise gently or ignore. Your own Hajj is your responsibility.
  • ❌ Women covering face in Ihram: Niqab is prohibited; use an umbrella or visor.
