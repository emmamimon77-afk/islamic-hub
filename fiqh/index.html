📖 Fiqh & Rulings

فَاسْأَلُوا أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ إِن كُنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ

"So ask the people of knowledge if you do not know." — Surah An-Nahl (16:43)

Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence) is the understanding and application of divine rulings derived from the Quran, Sunnah, consensus (ijma'), and analogical reasoning (qiyas). This section covers essential rulings related to Hajj, Umrah, and daily worship — presented with clarity, authenticity, and respect for scholarly differences.

🌱 Basic Rulings

Prerequisites for Hajj and Umrah: Financial ability, physical health, mahram for women, sincere intention, and freedom from immediately due debt.

📚 Sources of Fiqh

  • Primary: Quran, Sunnah (Sahih Hadith)
  • Secondary: Ijma' (consensus of scholars), Qiyas (analogical reasoning)
