📚 Level 2 of 3 — Intermediate Fiqh (Pillars, Obligations & Invalidators)

📖 Intermediate Fiqh Rulings

وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلَّهِ

"And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:196)

🏛️ 1. The Pillars (Arkan) of Hajj

If any pillar is missed, Hajj is invalid.

  1. Ihram — Entering the sacred state with intention at the Miqat.
  2. Wuquf at Arafah — Standing at the plain of Arafah on 9th Dhul-Hijjah, from noon until sunset.
  3. Tawaf al-Ifadah — Circumambulation of the Kaaba (also called Tawaf az-Ziyarah).
  4. Sa'i — Walking seven times between Safa and Marwah.
📜 HADITH
"The Prophet (ﷺ) said: 'Take your rituals from me.'" (Sahih Muslim)

✅ 2. The Obligations (Wajibat) of Hajj

If an obligation is missed, a sacrifice (dam) is required. Hajj remains valid.

  1. Entering Ihram from the designated Miqat.
  2. Standing at Arafah until sunset (for those present during the day).
  3. Spending the night at Muzdalifah.
  4. Spending the nights of Tashreeq (11th-13th) at Mina.
  5. Stoning the Jamarat (pillars) in Mina in order.
  6. Shaving or trimming the hair (Halq/Taqsir) after completing Hajj.
  7. Performing Tawaf al-Wada (Farewell Tawaf) before leaving Makkah.

❌ 3. What Invalidates Hajj or Umrah

  • Marital relations — Before Tawaf al-Ifadah, this invalidates Hajj completely.
  • Leaving Ihram intentionally before completing rituals.
  • Missing a pillar (rukn) — e.g., leaving Arafah before sunset.
  • Abandoning Hajj after entering Ihram without valid excuse (illness, force majeure).
📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:197)
الْحَجُّ أَشْهُرٌ مَّعْلُومَاتٌ ۚ فَمَن فَرَضَ فِيهِنَّ الْحَجَّ فَلَا رَفَثَ وَلَا فُسُوقَ وَلَا جِدَالَ فِي الْحَجِّ
Meaning: "Hajj is during known months. Whoever intends to perform Hajj therein — let there be no obscenity, no wickedness, no disputing during Hajj."

🐑 4. When a Sacrifice (Dam) is Required

  • Missing an obligation (wajib) — e.g., leaving Tawaf al-Wada.
  • Shaving head before sacrificing for Tamattu' or Qiran.
  • Wearing perfume or stitched clothing in Ihram (out of forgetfulness).
  • Killing game or cutting trees in the Haram.
📚 Types of Sacrifice:
  • Dam al-Tamattu': Required for Hajj al-Tamattu' (one sheep/goat or 1/7 of a cow/camel).
  • Dam al-Qiran: Required for Hajj al-Qiran (same as above).
  • Dam al-Jaz': For missing an obligation or committing a prohibited act.

🕌 5. Specific Rulings for Umrah

  • Umrah has only 4 rituals: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Halq/Taqsir.
  • No sacrifice required for Umrah alone (unless in Hajj months, then Tamattu' requires sacrifice).
  • Can be performed multiple times — each Umrah expiates sins between them.
  • Women's menstruation: If menstruation begins before Tawaf, she must wait until clean. Can perform Sa'i while menstruating (different opinion).
📜 HADITH
"Umrah to the next Umrah is an expiation for what is between them." (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)

⚠️ 6. Common Mistakes (Intermediate Level)

  • ❌ Leaving Arafah before sunset — Must stay until sunset unless physically unable.
  • ❌ Neglecting du'a at Arafah — The best du'a is on this day.
  • ❌ Stoning before Dhuhr on Tashreeq days — Stoning is after Dhuhr.
  • ❌ Thinking shaving is optional for men — Shaving (halq) is highly recommended.
  • ❌ Leaving Farewell Tawaf — Wajib; if missed, a sacrifice is required.
