🌱 Level 1 of 3 — Basic Fiqh (Prerequisites & Conditions)

📖 Basic Fiqh Rulings for Hajj & Umrah

وَلِلَّهِ عَلَى النَّاسِ حِجُّ الْبَيْتِ مَنِ اسْتَطَاعَ إِلَيْهِ سَبِيلًا

"And to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find a way." — Surah Al-Imran (3:97)

📋 1. Conditions That Make Hajj Obligatory

A Muslim is only required to perform Hajj if ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • ✅ Muslim: Only Muslims are obligated.
  • ✅ Adult (Baligh): Reached puberty. Children may perform Hajj but it is not obligatory.
  • ✅ Sane Mind: Free from mental illness that prevents understanding.
  • ✅ Free (not a slave): In Islamic history, slaves were not required.
  • 💰 Physically Able: Health permits the journey and rituals.
  • 💰 Financially Able: Sufficient funds beyond debts and family needs.
  • 👩 Mahram (for women): A woman must travel with a husband or male relative she cannot marry.
  • 🛡️ Safe Passage: The route to Makkah must be safe.
📜 EVIDENCE
لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا
"Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:286)

💰 2. Financial Prerequisites

  • Debt-free (for obligatory Hajj): Major debts must be cleared or arranged with creditors.
  • Sufficient funds for self: Transportation, accommodation, food, medical emergencies, return ticket.
  • Sufficient funds for dependents: Family left behind must have enough for living expenses while away.
  • Halal (lawful) earnings: Money used for Hajj must be from halal sources. Haram income invalidates the pilgrimage.
  • No intention of begging: A pilgrim cannot beg during Hajj. If funds run out, Hajj becomes questionable.
📖 QURAN
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَنْفِقُوا مِنْ طَيِّبَاتِ مَا كَسَبْتُمْ
"O you who believe, spend from the good things you have earned." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:267)

🤝 3. Social Prerequisites (Rights of Others)

  • Mahram for women: A woman must travel with her husband or a male relative she cannot marry.
  • No oppression (dhulm): If you have oppressed anyone — taken money, hurt reputation, physically harmed — Hajj is not accepted until you return rights or seek forgiveness.
  • Seeking forgiveness: Before departure, sincerely apologize to anyone you have wronged.
  • Good character: No arguing, insulting, raising voice, pushing, or showing arrogance during Hajj.
  • No showing off (riya'): Performing Hajj for photos, status, or social media invalidates sincerity.
📜 HADITH
"The Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand Muslims are safe." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

🕋 4. Spiritual Prerequisites (Rights of Allah)

  • Sincere intention (ikhlas): The heart must be free from seeking praise, wealth, or status.
  • Knowledge before action: Learning the rituals is essential before going.
  • Repentance (tawbah): Sincere turning away from all sins — major and minor.
  • Returning stolen property: If you have taken anything that does not belong to you, you must return it or be forgiven by the owner.
  • No arrogance: Competing in wealth (better hotels, clothes) or status during Hajj contradicts its spirit of humility.
📖 QURAN
إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ اللَّهُ مِنَ الْمُتَّقِينَ
"Allah only accepts from the righteous." — Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:27)

📝 Key Takeaway

Hajj is only accepted if performed with halal earnings, sincere intention, and in a state of purity from oppression (dhulm). The physical rituals alone are not enough — the heart must be prepared.

📚 Sources: Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Fiqh us Sunnah, Dar al-Iftaa
