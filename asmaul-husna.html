"And to Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them." — Surah Al-A'raf (7:180)

🤲 A Reminder

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Allah has ninety-nine names. Whoever memorizes them and acts upon them will enter Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari)

May Allah make us among those who know Him by His beautiful names and call upon Him with them. Ameen.
