📚 References & Sources

قُلْ إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ ٱللَّهَ فَٱتَّبِعُونِى يُحْبِبْكُمُ ٱللَّهُ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ

"Say, 'If you love Allah, then follow me; Allah will love you and forgive your sins. For Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.'" — Surah Ali 'Imran (3:31)

📖 Primary Sources
  • The Holy Quran — Madinah Mushaf (Tanzil.net, Quran.com)
  • Sahih al-Bukhari — Compiled by Imam Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Bukhari (d. 256 AH)
  • Sahih Muslim — Compiled by Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj (d. 261 AH)
  • Sunan Abi Dawud — Compiled by Imam Abu Dawud al-Sijistani (d. 275 AH)
  • Sunan al-Tirmidhi — Compiled by Imam Muhammad ibn Isa al-Tirmidhi (d. 279 AH)
  • Sunan al-Nasa'i — Compiled by Imam Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb al-Nasa'i (d. 303 AH)
  • Sunan Ibn Majah — Compiled by Imam Muhammad ibn Yazid Ibn Majah (d. 273 AH)
📚 Secondary Sources & Reference Works
  • Fiqh us-Sunnah — Sayyid Sabiq (Comprehensive fiqh reference for Hajj, Umrah, and daily worship)
  • Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) — Sa'id bin Ali bin Wahf al-Qahtani (Authentic du'as collection)
  • Rulings of Hajj and 'Umrah — Shaykh Muhammad Salih al-'Uthaymin
  • The Rites of Hajj and 'Umrah Booklet — Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah (Egyptian Fatwa House)
  • Tafsir Ibn Kathir — Imam Isma'il ibn Kathir (Quranic exegesis)
  • Al-Adhkar — Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf al-Nawawi (Compilation of authentic remembrances)
🌐 Online Resources & Verification Tools
  • Sunnah.com — Digital collection of authentic Hadith (Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah)
  • Quran.com — Digital mushaf with multiple translations and recitations
  • Tanzil.net — Verified Quranic text (Madinah Mushaf)
  • Dorar.net — Comprehensive Hadith verification (Arabic)
  • IslamWeb.net — Fatwas and Islamic rulings
  • Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah — Egyptian Fatwa House (Official Hajj/Umrah booklets)
📝 Specific Sources for Du'as & Rulings
  • 40 Rabbana Duas: Compiled from the Holy Quran, verified via Tanzil.net
  • Morning & Evening Adhkar: Verified from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Sunan al-Tirmidhi
  • After Salah Du'as: Based on authentic Sunnah (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim)
  • Protection Du'as: Ayatul Kursi, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, and authentic prophetic ruqyah
  • Health & Healing: Quranic healing verses (Ayat ash-Shifa) and prophetic du'as for the sick
  • Grief & Debt Relief: Du'as from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud
  • Family & Children Du'as: Quranic verses (Surah Al-Furqan 25:74, Surah Ibrahim 14:40-41) and authentic Hadith
  • Hajj & Umrah Du'as: Talbiyah, Arafah du'a, Tawaf du'as — verified from Sahih Hadith
  • Funeral Du'as: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud
✅ Verification Note
  • All Quranic verses have been verified against the Madinah Mushaf (Tanzil.net / Quran.com)
  • All Hadith references have been verified via Sunnah.com — only Sahih (authentic) or Hasan (good) gradings are used
  • Where multiple narrations exist, the strongest (Sahih) is preferred
  • Rulings on Hajj and Umrah are based on the Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, and Hanbali schools, with preference given to strongest evidence
  • Abrogated (mansukh) narrations have been avoided
  • Weak (da'if) or fabricated (mawdu') narrations have been excluded entirely
