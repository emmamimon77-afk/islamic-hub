🕋 Hajj: Advanced Fiqh & Spiritual Realities

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:196)
وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلَّهِ
"And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah."

📖 1. The Three Types of Hajj (Advanced)

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:196) — TAMATTU'
فَمَنْ تَمَتَّعَ بِالْعُمْرَةِ إِلَى الْحَجِّ فَمَا اسْتَيْسَرَ مِنَ الْهَدْيِ
"And whoever performs Umrah during the Hajj months, then (offers) whatever is easy from the sacrificial animal."

Scholars classify Hajj into three forms. Each has different requirements and rulings.

🕋 Hajj al-Ifrad (Single Hajj)

Intention: Hajj only, without Umrah. Pilgrim enters Ihram for Hajj alone.

Ruling: No sacrifice (hady) required. Preferred by some scholars for those not bringing an animal.

🕋🤲 Hajj al-Tamattu' (Most Common)

Intention: Umrah first (during Hajj months), then exits Ihram, then enters Ihram again for Hajj on 8th Dhul-Hijjah.

Ruling: Sacrifice (hady) required. This is what the Prophet (ﷺ) commanded for those not bringing animals.

📜 Evidence: The Prophet (ﷺ) said to his companions: "If I had known my affair beforehand, I would have brought the sacrificial animal and performed Tamattu'." (Bukhari)

🕋🕋 Hajj al-Qiran (Combined)

Intention: Umrah and Hajj together in a single Ihram. Pilgrim stays in Ihram until completing both.

Ruling: Sacrifice (hady) required. Considered more virtuous by some, but more difficult.

📜 SCHOLARLY NOTE
Which is best? For most pilgrims today, Hajj al-Tamattu' is recommended because the Prophet (ﷺ) ordered it for those not bringing animals. (Source: Ibn Uthaymin, Rulings of Hajj)

⚖️ 2. Major Differences of Opinion (Madhhab Comparisons)

📍 Stoning on 10th Dhul-Hijjah

  • Hanafi: Must stone after sunrise.
  • Shafi'i & Hanbali: Allowed from after Fajr until sunset.
  • Maliki: Preferred after sunrise but permissible before.

📍 Night in Muzdalifah

  • Hanafi: Wajib (obligatory) to spend most of the night.
  • Shafi'i & Maliki: Sunnah, not obligatory.
  • Hanbali: Staying until midnight is sufficient.

📍 Farewell Tawaf (Tawaf al-Wada)

  • All four schools: Wajib (obligatory) except for menstruating women.
  • If missed: Hanafi & Hanbali require a sacrifice (dam); Shafi'i & Maliki say make up if possible.
📜 PRINCIPLE
When scholars differ, follow the opinion that brings you closest to Allah with clear evidence. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Differences in my Ummah are a mercy." (Reported by al-Bayhaqi)

💎 3. The Spiritual Secrets of Hajj (Tazkiyah)

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-HAJJ (22:27)
وَأَذِّن فِي النَّاسِ بِالْحَجِّ يَأْتُوكَ رِجَالًا وَعَلَىٰ كُلِّ ضَامِرٍ يَأْتِينَ مِن كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيقٍ
"And proclaim to the people the Hajj — they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel, from every distant pass."

▪️ Ihram = Death Preparation

The two white sheets resemble the shroud. Hajj is a rehearsal for meeting Allah. The pilgrim leaves behind status, wealth, and identity.

▪️ Tawaf = The Heart's Circumambulation

Just as the body circles the Kaaba, the heart should circle Allah alone — free from love of dunya (world), fame, and false gods.

▪️ Sa'i = The Struggle of Hagar

Walking between Safa and Marwah commemorates Hagar's desperate search for water for her son Isma'il. It teaches: Trust Allah, but take action.

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:158)
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ
"Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah."

▪️ Arafah = The Day of Standing

Wuquf at Arafah represents the Day of Judgment. Pilgrims stand, sweat, cry, and beg — just as all humanity will stand before Allah.

▪️ Stoning the Jamarat = Fighting Shaytan

Each pebble is a rejection of Satan's whispers. The pilgrim declares: "I follow Allah, not my ego or Shaytan."

📜 Ibn al-Qayyim (rahimahullah) said: "Hajj is a journey to Allah. Every ritual has a meaning. The pilgrim returns from Hajj reborn — sins erased, heart purified."

🌍 4. Hajj & the End of Times (Eschatology)

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-IMRAN (3:185)
كُلُّ نَفْسٍ ذَائِقَةُ الْمَوْتِ ۗ وَإِنَّمَا تُوَفَّوْنَ أُجُورَكُمْ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ
"Every soul will taste death. And you will only be given your full reward on the Day of Resurrection."

📜 Prophecies about Hajj in the Last Days

  • The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "The Hour will not be established until Hajj is abandoned." (Bukhari)
  • "A time will come when people perform Hajj without scholars — they go only for trade and tourism." (Ibn Hibban)
  • "The Kaaba will be destroyed by a thin-legged man from Abyssinia." (Ahmad)

🔮 Signs Before the End

  • Hajj becomes extremely expensive or dangerous
  • People go for show (riya') and photos, not for Allah
  • The wealthy perform Hajj repeatedly while the poor cannot go even once
  • The Kaaba is stripped of its covering and later destroyed
🤲 DU'A TO BE AMONG THE RIGHTEOUS
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ لَا تَجْعَلْ آخِرَ ٱلْعَهْدِ مِنْ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتِ، وَٱرْزُقْنِي حَجَّ بَيْتِكَ قَبْلَ أَنْ لَا حَجَّ
"O Allah, do not make this my last visit to this House, and grant me Hajj to Your House before there is no Hajj."

🩺 5. Advanced Rulings: Sick, Elderly & Deceased

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:196) — EXEMPTION
فَمَن كَانَ مِنكُم مَّرِيضًا أَوْ بِهِ أَذًى مِّن رَّأْسِهِ فَفِدْيَةٌ مِّن صِيَامٍ أَوْ صَدَقَةٍ أَوْ نُسُكٍ
"And whoever among you is ill or has an ailment of the head (making shaving necessary) — then a ransom of fasting or charity or sacrifice."

▪️ Performing Hajj on Behalf of Someone Else

  • Living but permanently unable (illness/age): Allowed — you may pay someone to perform Hajj on their behalf.
  • Deceased who did not perform Hajj: Allowed — the heir may perform Hajj or pay someone.
  • Living and able: Not allowed — each person must perform for themselves.

▪️ If a Pilgrim Dies During Hajj

  • They are considered a martyr (shahid) — no need to wash the body (remains in Ihram state).
  • Their Hajj is accepted as if completed.
  • The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever dies during Hajj, neither will they be resurrected nor accounted (for that journey's sin)." (Tabarani)
📜 Evidence: A woman asked the Prophet (ﷺ): "My father is old and cannot ride. Can I perform Hajj for him?" He said: "Yes, perform Hajj for him." (Bukhari)

📱 6. Modern Fiqh Issues (Advanced)

📸 Taking Photos/Selfies During Hajj

  • Ruling: Disliked (makruh) during rituals — distracts from sincerity and worship.
  • Prohibited: If it leads to showing off (riya') or delays wajib acts.

💉 Vaccinations & Medical Needs

  • Injections (non-nutritional): Permitted — do not break Ihram unless they contain perfume.
  • Blood tests, IV fluids: Permitted if medically necessary.

🚗 Using Wheelchairs & Electric Carts

  • Permitted for those with genuine need (elderly, sick).
  • Not preferred for healthy people — walking Tawaf and Sa'i is more virtuous.

💰 Paying Someone to Stone on Your Behalf

  • Only permitted if you are unable (sick, elderly, in danger).
  • Proxy must perform stoning in the same day and pillar order.

⚠️ 7. Common Advanced-Level Mistakes

  • ❌ Thinking Tamattu' is only for certain nationalities: Tamattu' is for all pilgrims not bringing a sacrifice animal — the Prophet (ﷺ) ordered it.
  • ❌ Believing stoning the pillars literally stones Shaytan: It is symbolic — a declaration of rejection of evil.
  • ❌ Neglecting the spiritual meaning: Rituals without understanding become mere physical movement.
  • ❌ Correcting others harshly: If you see someone making a mistake, advise gently or ignore. The goal is your own Hajj, not judging others.
  • ❌ Ignoring women's specific rulings: Menstruation, niqab in Ihram, and mahram rules are often misunderstood.
