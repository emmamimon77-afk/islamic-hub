🕋 The Complete Hajj Guide

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:196)
وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلَّهِ
"And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah."

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam — an obligation once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able. This guide will walk you through every step, from preparation to completion, based on authentic sources including Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah and Sahih Hadith.

📋 Prerequisites for Hajj & Umrah

Hajj is only obligatory and valid when these conditions are met. Missing any spiritual or social prerequisite may invalidate the pilgrimage or deprive one of its reward.

💰 Financial Prerequisite

  • Debt-free: All major debts (loans, credit cards, borrowed money) must be cleared or a reliable arrangement made with creditors. Hajj is not obligatory if debt prevents supporting one's family.
  • Sufficient funds for self: Transportation, accommodation, food, medical emergencies, and return ticket home.
  • Sufficient funds for dependents left at home: This means your family (spouse, children, elderly parents under your care) must have enough money for food, rent, utilities, and emergencies while you are away. You cannot leave them in hardship. Example: If your family needs $2000/month for living expenses and you will be gone for 1 month, you must leave $2000+ for them.
  • Halal (lawful) earnings: The money used for Hajj must be from halal sources. Haram income (interest, gambling, stolen goods, bribery) invalidates the pilgrimage. Allah accepts only the pure.
  • No intention of begging: A pilgrim cannot beg during Hajj. If funds run out, Hajj becomes invalid if one resorts to asking others.

🤝 Social Prerequisite (Rights of Others)

  • Mahram for women: A woman must travel with her husband or a male relative she cannot marry (father, brother, son, uncle). The mahram must also be financially capable and trustworthy.
  • No oppression (dhulm): If you have oppressed anyone — taken their money, hurt their reputation, physically harmed them — Hajj is not accepted until you return their rights or seek their forgiveness.
  • Seeking forgiveness: Before departure, you must sincerely apologize to anyone you have wronged. Example: A business partner you cheated, a neighbor you harmed, a family member you abandoned.
  • Good character with pilgrims: No arguing, insulting, raising voice, pushing, or showing anger. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Hajj is Arafah" (meaning patience and remembrance).
  • No showing off (riya'): Performing Hajj for photos, status, or "Hajji" title invalidates sincerity. The intention must be only Allah.

🕋 Spiritual Prerequisite (Rights of Allah)

  • Sincere intention (ikhlas): The heart must be free from seeking praise, wealth, or marriage. Only Allah's pleasure matters.
  • Knowledge before action: Learning the rituals (Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Arafah, Muzdalifah, Mina, stoning, sacrifice, farewell tawaf) is wajib (obligatory) before going. Ignorance leads to mistakes that may require sacrifice.
  • Repentance (tawbah): Sincere turning away from all sins — major (zina, alcohol, gambling) and minor (backbiting, lying, wasting time). Without repentance, the journey may be physical only without spiritual reward.
  • Returning stolen property: If you have taken anything that does not belong to you (money, goods, time from employer), you must return it or be forgiven by the owner. Example: An employee who wasted work hours secretly must compensate.
  • No arrogance or worldly competition: Competing in wealth (better hotels, cars, clothes) or status during Hajj contradicts its spirit of humility and equality.

📚 Sources: Dar al-Iftaa al-Misriyyah (The Rites of Hajj and 'Umrah Booklet, pages 8-12), Fiqh us Sunnah (Sayyid Sabiq, Volume 5), and rulings from Shaykh Ibn Uthaymin (Rulings of Hajj and 'Umrah).

⚠️ Critical Warning: If any financial or social prerequisite is missing — especially debt, oppressing others, or leaving dependents in need — Hajj may be physically performed but spiritually rejected. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever performs Hajj and does not commit any obscenity or transgression will return like the day his mother bore him." The opposite is also true.

📜 The 4 Pillars of Hajj (Arkan)

If any pillar is missed, Hajj is invalid.

  1. Ihram — Entering the state of consecration with the intention of Hajj.
  2. Wuquf at Arafah — Standing at the plain of Arafah on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah.
  3. Tawaf al-Ifadah — Circumambulation of the Kaaba (also called Tawaf az-Ziyarah).
  4. Sa'i — Walking seven times between Safa and Marwah.

✅ The 7 Obligations of Hajj (Wajibat)

If missed, a sacrifice (dam) is required.

  1. Entering Ihram from the designated Miqat.
  2. Standing at Arafah until sunset (for those present during the day).
  3. Spending the night at Muzdalifah.
  4. Spending the nights of Tashreeq (11th-13th) at Mina.
  5. Stoning the Jamarat (pillars) in Mina.
  6. Shaving or trimming the hair after completing Hajj.
  7. Performing Tawaf al-Wada (Farewell Tawaf) before leaving Makkah.

📜 Hadith on Hajj

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever performs Hajj for the sake of Allah and does not commit any obscenity or transgression will return (free from sin) as the day his mother bore him."

— Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim
