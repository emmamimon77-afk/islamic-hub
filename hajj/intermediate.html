📚 Level 2 of 3 — Intermediate (Step-by-Step Rituals)

🕋 Hajj: Complete Step-by-Step Guide

📖 QURAN · SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2:203)
وَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ فِي أَيَّامٍ مَعْدُودَاتٍ
"And remember Allah during the appointed days."

📅 Hajj Timeline: 8th - 13th Dhul-Hijjah

Day 1: 8th → Mina Day 2: 9th → Arafah Night 9th → Muzdalifah Day 3: 10th → Rami + Sacrifice + Shave Days 4-5: 11th-12th → Tashreeq Before Leaving → Farewell Tawaf

📍 Day 1: 8th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm at-Tarwiyah)

1. Enter Ihram from your location (or Miqat)

  • Perform ghusl (full bath), apply non-scented oil, wear Ihram garments (men: two white sheets; women: modest dress, uncover face and hands)
  • Make intention: "Labbaik Allahumma Hajjan" (O Allah, I answer Your call for Hajj)
  • Recite Talbiyah frequently:
    📜 الْقُرْآن / ARABIC
    لَبَّيْكَ ٱللَّٰهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ ٱلْحَمْدَ وَٱلنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَٱلْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
    🔊 TRANSLITERATION
    Labbaik Allahumma labbaik, labbaik la sharika laka labbaik, innal hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.
    🌍 MEANING
    "Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Verily all praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty, You have no partner."

2. Travel to Mina

  • Stay overnight in Mina (tent city)
  • Pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha (shortened but not combined)
  • Spend the night in worship, dhikr, and du'a
📖 Du'a for travel (to Mina):
📜 الْقُرْآن / ARABIC
ٱللَّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ. سُبْحَانَ ٱلَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ
🔊 TRANSLITERATION
Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar. Subhanalladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin. Wa inna ila rabbina lamunqalibun.
🌍 MEANING
"Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. Glory be to the One Who has subjected this (mode of transport) to us, for we could never have mastered it ourselves. And indeed, to our Lord we will return."

📍 Day 2: 9th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm Arafah) — The Most Important Day

3. Depart for Arafah after sunrise

  • Recite Talbiyah, Takbir, and Tahleel (La ilaha illa Allah)
  • Stay within Arafah boundaries until sunset

4. Wuquf (Standing) at Arafah

  • This is the Hajj! The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Hajj is Arafah."
  • Face the Qibla, raise hands, make sincere du'a for yourself, family, Ummah
  • Combine Dhuhr and Asr prayers (shortened) at the time of Dhuhr
  • Spend the entire day in supplication, crying, and seeking forgiveness
💎 Best du'a on Arafah (the Prophet's du'a):
📜 الْقُرْآن / ARABIC
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ ٱلْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
🔊 TRANSLITERATION
La ilaha illa Allah wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul mulku wa lahul hamdu, wa huwa ala kulli shay'in qadir.
🌍 MEANING
"There is no god worthy of worship except Allah alone, without any partners. To Him belongs all sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things competent."
📜 Hadith: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Khayru du'a'i du'a'u yawmi Arafah" — "The best du'a is the du'a of the day of Arafah."
— (Sunan al-Tirmidhi)

📍 Night of 9th-10th: Muzdalifah

5. Depart Arafah after sunset

  • Travel calmly to Muzdalifah (no pushing, no rushing)
  • Pray Maghrib and Isha together (combined, shortened Isha)
  • Collect 7 or 49 or 70 small pebbles for stoning (Jamarat)
  • Spend the night under the open sky in worship
  • Do not leave Muzdalifah until after Fajr prayer
📖 Du'a in Muzdalifah:
📜 الْقُرْآن / ARABIC
ٱللَّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ هَٰذِهِ ٱللَّيْلَةِ وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا
🔊 TRANSLITERATION
Allahumma inni as'aluka khayra hadhihil laylati wa khayra ma fiha, wa a'udhu bika min sharriha wa sharri ma fiha.
🌍 MEANING
"O Allah, I ask You for the goodness of this night and the goodness of what is in it, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of what is in it."

📍 Day 3: 10th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm an-Nahr) — Eid al-Adha

6. Stoning of the Great Jamarah (Jamarat al-Aqabah)

  • After Fajr, return to Mina before sunrise
  • Throw 7 consecutive pebbles at the largest pillar (Jamarat al-Aqabah)
  • Say "Allahu Akbar" with each throw
  • Stop reciting Talbiyah after this stoning

7. Sacrifice (Hady) — For those performing Hajj al-Tamattu' or Qiran

  • Sacrifice a sheep, goat, cow (1/7 share), or camel (1/7 share)
  • Meat is distributed to the poor (you may eat some)
  • Can be done by proxy (coupon system in modern Hajj)

8. Shave or Trim Hair (Halq or Taqsir)

  • Men: Shave head completely (halq) or trim (taqsir) — shaving is better
  • Women: Cut a fingertip's length from their hair
  • After this, you are partially released from Ihram (can wear normal clothes, use perfume, but marital relations remain prohibited until Tawaf al-Ifadah)

📍 Tawaf al-Ifadah (The Essential Circumambulation)

  • Go to Makkah (Masjid al-Haram)
  • Perform Tawaf: 7 circuits around the Kaaba
  • Perform Sa'i: 7 walks between Safa and Marwah (if not already done for Umrah)
  • After this, all restrictions of Ihram are lifted — including marital relations
📖 Du'a during Tawaf (between Yemeni Corner and Black Stone):
📜 الْقُرْآن / ARABIC
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي ٱلْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ
🔊 TRANSLITERATION
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina adhaban-nar.
🌍 MEANING
"Our Lord, give us in this world good, and in the Hereafter good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire." — (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)

📍 Days of Tashreeq: 11th, 12th (and 13th) Dhul-Hijjah

9. Stoning of the 3 Jamarat

  • Each day after Dhuhr, stone all three pillars in order:
  • Small Jamarah: 7 pebbles → "Allahu Akbar" each throw
  • Middle Jamarah: 7 pebbles
  • Great Jamarah: 7 pebbles
  • Make du'a after Small and Middle pillars (face Qibla, raise hands)

10. Leaving Mina

  • You may leave Mina on the 12th after stoning (before sunset)
  • If you stay until the 13th, stone all three pillars again on the 13th after Dhuhr
  • Staying the 13th is better (following the Prophet's example)

📍 Farewell Tawaf (Tawaf al-Wada)

  • Before leaving Makkah, perform 7 circuits around the Kaaba
  • No Sa'i required unless you wish
  • Pray 2 rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim (if possible)
  • Drink Zamzam water and make du'a for forgiveness
  • Exit the Masjid walking backward (out of respect) — not required but a loving tradition
⚠️ Important: Farewell Tawaf is wajib (obligatory) for all pilgrims. Menstruating women are exempt. Do not leave Makkah without it.

💡 Practical Tips for Intermediate Pilgrims

  • Save energy: Do not over-exert in the first days. Hajj is a marathon, not a sprint.
  • Hydrate constantly: Zamzam water is available everywhere — drink often.
  • Use a wrist watch or phone: Time management is critical, especially in Arafah and Muzdalifah.
  • Wear ID bracelet: In case you get lost in crowds (common in Mina).
  • Carry a small backpack: With prayer mat, snacks, water, medications, and a copy of du'as.
  • Do not push during stoning: Throw from a distance if crowded. Hitting the pillar is not required — only throwing into the basin.

⚠️ Common Mistakes at This Level

  • ❌ Leaving Arafah before sunset: You must stay until sunset (unless physically unable).
  • ❌ Neglecting du'a on Arafah: The best du'a is on this day — don't waste it on photos or chatting.
  • ❌ Stoning before Dhuhr on Tashreeq days: Stoning is after Dhuhr (unless extreme crowding is feared).
  • ❌ Thinking shaving is optional for men: Shaving (halq) is highly recommended; trimming (taqsir) is allowed but less reward.
  • ❌ Leaving Farewell Tawaf: Many forget or ignore it. It is wajib — a sacrifice (dam) is required if missed.
